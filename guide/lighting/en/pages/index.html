Sun Forge 2D Lighting Documentation

Hover a field in an Inspector for what it does. These pages hold what no field shows:

Page Look up
Lighting.md The Light Source and your Light 2D lights, the style materials, the properties you tune on a copy, the textures a sprite is lit from and how to bring your own or pick the colors that glow or shine like metal, what each setting costs and the buttons that set them at once, and setting up a scene from a script
Custom-Shaders.md Lighting a Shader Graph or shader of your own with the scene's style

The Lit Scene sample, which the Package Manager offers under Samples, is a lit scene to open and take apart. Its README.md says how it is set up.

A script in an assembly definition of your own references KonradRzonca.SunForge.Lighting, and an editor script also KonradRzonca.SunForge.Lighting.Editor. The components are in the KonradRzonca.SunForge namespace, and the editor types in KonradRzonca.SunForge.Editor.

These pages are also online at https://sunforgetools.com/guide/lighting/0.3/en/, with every menu, component and Scene view row and what each does.
