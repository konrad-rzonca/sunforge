Styles

Switch a scene between the four styles, and choose what each quality level draws.

On this page
  • Materials
  • Style Quality

Materials

The package has four styles: Regular, Cinematic, Flat and Realistic. To change a scene's style, select its Lighting Setup and click a style under Style.

Light A Room

Each style has an Indoor variant, for a room:

  1. Select the scene's Lighting Setup. Under Style, click Indoor, then the style you want.
  2. For each lamp, fire or window, choose a starting look under GameObject > Sun Forge > Light Source, such as Lamps > Lantern, Fire > Fireplace or Windows and Doors > Window.
  3. To make the room brighter or darker, set Sun And Moon Brightness, the light through the windows, and Sky Brightness, the light in the shade, under Global Light. The room still changes with Time Of Day.
  4. Under Regular, Cinematic or Realistic, to have metal reflect your room, import a panorama of it with Texture Shape set to Cube, and assign it as the Lighting Setup's Environment. Under Flat, metal reflects no image and no sky.

Roles

Every style has one material per role:

Role For Material
Sprite Actors, props and effects that stand up SunForge_<Style>_SpriteLit
Ground Terrain, roads, decals and other flat art drawn top-down, and a side view's floors and walls SunForge_<Style>_Ground
Ground Clutter Stones, tufts, grass and other small solids lying on the ground, drawn from the same angle as your standing sprites SunForge_<Style>_GroundClutter

A sprite draws the material of the Lighting Setup's style for its Role, so it changes when the style does. A sprite whose Lighting Style Renderer names a Material draws that material under every style. It still takes the sun, sky and weather, the lights, cast shadows, mist and post-processing from the scene's Lighting Setup.

The Unity Lights Material

The Unity Lights material, SunForge_UnityLights_SpriteLit, belongs to no style. Unity's 2D lights light it under the 2D Renderer, and under the Universal Renderer URP's lights light it as an upright sprite in a scene whose up is +Y, such as a side view or a 3D scene. It reads a sprite's normal, mask and emission textures, and takes no mist, cast shadows or ground cover from the Lighting Setup.

Style Quality

A style's Quality section sets at which quality levels the style draws its costly options: Light From Lit Ground, which Flat does not have, and Exact Shadows On Sprites. To change one:

  1. Select your Lighting Setup and open the section named for its style, below Values. On one of Sun Forge's styles, click Edit A Copy in the section's header.
  2. In that section, open Quality.
  3. Beside the option, pick Every Level, Never, or From and a level. From High, for example, draws the option at High and at each level listed after it in Project Settings > Quality.

If you build Addressables or asset bundles, clear their build cache after you change a style's Quality.
