The showcase

How to open the showcase scene, what it needs, and how to remove it.

On this page
  • Requirements
  • Open The Showcase
  • Remove The Showcase

A playable forest scene where every actor and prop is Sun Forge bake output, and so is every effect except the grass and the wisps that come out at night. A control panel switches each feature, and the sample keeps the 3D models the art was baked from and the presets that baked it, so you can change a preset and rebake.

Requirements

  • Sun Forge and Sun Forge 2D Lighting, which install with the showcase, and everything Sun Forge's README lists under Requirements.
  • Installing the showcase also enables the UIElements and Vector Graphics built-in packages.
  • For an Android or iOS build, give the quality level that platform uses its own copy of the URP asset, and set Render Scale to 0.5 on that copy. In an Android or iOS player, the scene's cast shadows draw at 16 texels a unit, which Phone Pixels Per Unit on the Cast Shadow Field Controller object under Features sets.
  • On Windows, keep the project folder path at 110 characters or fewer. In a longer one, some of the sample's files go over Windows' path limit and fail to import.

Open The Showcase

Choose Tools > Sun Forge > Open Showcase. The first time, it downloads Sun Forge Showcase if your project does not have it, imports the sample into Assets/Samples/Sun Forge Showcase/<version>/Showcase/ and opens its scene. The README.md in that folder says how to play the scene and rebake its art.

Remove The Showcase

Delete the imported Assets/Samples/Sun Forge Showcase folder, then remove Sun Forge Showcase in the Package Manager. The sample's scene and prefabs use that package's scripts, so removing it first leaves them with missing scripts.
