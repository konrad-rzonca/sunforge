Baked sprites in a scene

Which material a baked prefab draws with, how it follows the scene's style, and where its particles sort.

A new bake's sprites draw the material of the style the scene's Lighting Setup has. To draw one material in every scene, pick it as the bake's Prefab Material (Bake tab, Advanced view) and bake again. Make a Material for This Bake in that row's menu makes one of your own to tune.

  • A sprite with an enabled Baked Renderer Binding carries its bake's camera elevation and height range, which the style material's rows change only on sprites without one. Its glow is its bake's times the material's Emission Scale. The light a bake adds for Glow Light brightens with a style material's Emission Scale, and with a material of your own at the next bake (Tune a style).
  • A renderer of your own that draws baked sprites needs a Baked Renderer Binding, or it reads their heights at the material's range. Check Setup reports it.
  • A body on Unity's own sprite material shows no hit flash.
  • For Unity's 2D lights, add a Shadow Caster 2D to a baked prefab's root. Its shadows fall on the Unity Lights material.
  • The mist does not cover marker glows and trails, or shadow sprites whose Material (Archetypes tab, SHADOW, Advanced view) uses a shader other than Sun Forge/Hand-Drawn Shadow.
  • A baked shadow draws one Order in Layer below its body. Give the ground under it a lower one than both.
  • A Scale X of -1 on a parent does not mirror a baked shadow, so it still falls away from the sun. Outside a Cast Shadow Field, a shadow whose Material (Archetypes tab, SHADOW, Advanced view) uses another shader mirrors with its parent.

Baked Particles In A Scene

A prefab's particles draw over its whole body, even the parts of the model in front of them, such as a lantern's bars around its flame.

  • To draw them behind the body, move the prefab's Particles child up. They sort at that child's position.
  • A prefab baked from particles alone, such as a campfire flame, sorts by where it stands, like a character. Place a flame on a campfire's position or just in front of it to draw it over the campfire.
  • For a fire on a raised prop such as a torch, bake the two together, so the fire also lights the prop, shaded by the prop's own shape. Or set Origin Below Ground (Advanced view) to minus the prop's height and place the fire at the prop's base.
  • A looping flame baked on its own brings its own Glow light. To use a prop's light instead, turn off the flame's Glow Light and add its Particles child to Glow Renderers on the Light Source of the prop's Glow child.
  • An effect a script spawns in the air, such as a hit spark at a character's chest, sorts by the point where it appears, so it draws behind that character. To draw it in front, set the Order in Layer of its Particles child above the character's.
