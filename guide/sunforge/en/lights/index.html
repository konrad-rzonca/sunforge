Lights

Add a Light Source, shape its light, and keep using your Light 2D lights.

On this page
  • Add A Light
  • Shape A Light
  • Use Your Light 2D Lights
  • How Many Lights A Camera Lights
  • A Sprite's Own Glow Light

A Light Source lights the styled materials under both URP renderers. Its pool lies on the ground below it.

Add A Light

  1. Choose GameObject > Sun Forge > Light Source > Light. In the same submenu, Spot Light, Line Light and Rectangle Light light a cone, a line and a rectangle, and Fire, Lamps, Magic and Windows and Doors hold starting looks, such as a torch, a street lamp or a window.
  2. Move it to where the light shows, such as onto a lamp's flame.
  3. With the light selected, drag the dot above it in the Scene view to set Height Above Ground: the light rises or sinks and its pool stays where it is. The other handles set Ground Radius, Inner Radius, the size and the spot angles. Point at a handle to see its name and value.

To make an object you already have into a light, such as a lamp prefab, use Add Component > Sun Forge > Lighting > Light Source.

A 3D Point or Spot Light does not light styled sprites. To give one a Light Source, select it and click Add a Sun Forge Light Here at the top of its Inspector.

Shape A Light

To get Do
A spot Lower Outer Spot Angle, then drag the Aim handle, the dot with an arc past the pool's edge, to aim it
A pool that reaches farther Raise Intensity. Ground Radius sets where the light ends
A line or a rectangle of light, such as a tube lamp or a glowing panel Set Shape
A light that flickers like fire Set Kind to Flame. It flickers in Play mode. For a flame sprite of your own to flicker with it, add the sprite's renderer to Glow Renderers

Use Your Light 2D Lights

Your Light 2D lights keep lighting the Unity Lights material. To light your styled sprites with them too:

  1. Choose GameObject > Sun Forge > Set Up for Lighting and click Apply. Each Light 2D that targets every sorting layer gets a Light Source that it drives, so scripts and animations that change the Light 2D change the Light Source too. For one light, or one you add later, select it and click Use This Light with Sun Forge at the top of its Inspector.
  2. For a Light 2D that leaves a sorting layer out, select the Lighting Setup and click Fix beside the finding that names the light.
  3. Set each Light Source's Height Above Ground to how high its Light 2D hangs above the ground. The light stays at the Light 2D, and on styled sprites its pool lies on the ground below it, so the pool looks flattened, like the ground in your art. On side-view art, set a small Height Above Ground instead (Side view).

How Many Lights A Camera Lights

A camera lights up to 512 lights, or 128 in a WebGL 2 player. A light the main camera sees and leaves out shows a gray bulb in the Scene view. To keep one lit, turn on its Is Prioritized under Special Cases.

A Sprite's Own Glow Light

A Glow light is a Light Source that Sun Forge made for a sprite's glowing parts. A sprite Sun Forge wrote textures for gets one from Add in the Glow Light row (Glow, Surfaces and Edges, on its Lighting Style Renderer). The two Glow values under Values on the Lighting Setup set how it lights its own sprite, and the light's Light On Its Object Shift and Glow Parts Light Scale, under Special Cases, change that for one sprite.

Where a sprite's material is of another style than the scene's, such as a Flat material in a Regular scene, the material's style sets how its Glow light lights it, and the Lighting Setup's two Glow values do not change it. Realistic counts as Regular here, and a style of your own as the style you made it from. The light's Light On Its Object Shift and Glow Parts Light Scale still change such a sprite.
