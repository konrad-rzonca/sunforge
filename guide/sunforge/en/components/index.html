Add Component › Sun Forge

  • Pixel Sort Character

    Inspector

    Sorts a character against each part of a prop it stands in, while Per-Pixel Prop Sorting is on.

    Characters in props › Correcting One Prefab

    Inspector settings

    Automatic DepthSplits props at the front edge of the character's collider. Turn it off to type the depth yourself.
    DepthWorld units below the character's feet where props split around it. The part of a prop below that line draws in front of the character.

Add Component › Sun Forge › Animation

  • Facing

    Inspector

    Holds the direction an object faces and shows its sprites for that direction.

    Inspector settings

    FacingThe direction on screen the object faces. Zero faces the rest direction.
    AngleThe direction on screen, in degrees counterclockwise from right. The actor turns to the nearest direction its bake rendered.

  • Flipbook Animator Link

    Inspector

    Starts a baked particle flipbook's loop and turns it with the actor it sits under.

  • Flipbook Player

    Inspector

    Plays a baked actor's animations without an Animator, for scenes with many actors.

    Inspector settings

    Baked DataThe baked data it plays. Empty uses the baked prefab's data.
    ParticlesPlays the bake's particle flipbook instead of the body's animations.

Add Component › Sun Forge › Baked

  • Baked Prefab

    Inspector

    Links a baked prefab's root to its bake and lets a rebake keep the components you added.

    Inspector settings

    Baked DataThe baked data this prefab reads. A bake sets it. On a root you build yourself, set it here.

  • Baked Renderer Binding

    Inspector

    Links a renderer to its bake so Sun Forge shaders light its sprites with that bake's settings.

    Inspector settings

    Baked DataThe bake this renderer's sprites came from, whose values its shaders read. The bake sets it.
    Sprite KindWhich of the bake's sprites this renderer draws, such as the body or a shadow.
    Height Above GroundHow far above the ground this sprite stands, in world units, such as a lamp on a table or a bird in flight. Its light, shade, cast shadows and the grass drawn over it are read at that height. It does not move the sprite: place it there yourself.
    Split Around CharactersSplits this prop around each Pixel Sort Character inside it, while Per-Pixel Prop Sorting is on in Project Settings > Sun Forge. A character you made yourself needs that component too.

  • Baked Shadow Sync

    Keeps a baked actor's shadows on the same facing and animation frame as its body.

    Inspector settings

    BodyThe body renderer the shadows follow. Empty uses the Sprite Renderer on this GameObject.
    Ground ShadowThe ground shadow renderer, or a shadow you drew, falling away from the sun. Empty if there is none.
    Ellipse ShadowThe ellipse shadow renderer. Empty if there is none.
    Baked DataThe baked data the shadow frames come from. Empty uses the baked prefab's data.

Add Component › Sun Forge › Gameplay

  • Camera Follow

    Inspector

    Makes a camera follow a target smoothly at any frame rate.

    Inspector settings

    TargetThe actor the view frames. On the main camera, an actor with a Camera Target fills it in Play mode while it is empty.
    Target OffsetThe point the camera centers on, in world units from the target's root.
    Smooth TimeSeconds the view takes to settle behind a moving target.
    Teleport DistanceJump distance in world units above which the view cuts to the target instead of sliding.

  • Camera Target

    Makes the main camera follow this actor in Play mode, unless another script is on the camera, such as a Cinemachine Brain.

  • Health

    Inspector

    Takes damage from shots and strikes, and shows a health bar over the actor while it is damaged.

    Inspector settings

    Max HealthHit points the actor starts with.
    Show BarShow a health bar over the actor while it is damaged. It hides again at zero health.
    At Zero HealthKeep Standing keeps the actor in the scene for your own script to handle. Die And Deactivate plays the Die entry, when the bake has one, then deactivates the actor.
    DamagedRaised on every hit that lands, with the damage and the direction the hit traveled.
    DepletedRaised once, on the hit that brings health to zero.

  • Melee Attacker

    Walks up to the nearest actor with Movement and strikes it, playing the Play Once entry named Attack.

    Inspector settings

    TargetThe actor it chases. Empty chases the nearest actor with Movement. It stands while that actor is inactive or out of health, or while that actor's Health is disabled.
    SpeedWorld units per second. 0 uses the baked walk's speed, or 1.6 with no baked walk. At the baked speed the feet stay planted walking left or right.
    ReachHow close its collider gets to the target's before it stops and strikes, in world units. A strike lands on a target up to 0.25 farther away.
    DamageHit points a strike removes from the target's Health.
    Cooldown SecondsSeconds between strikes.

  • Movement

    Inspector

    Walks with WASD or the arrow keys, facing the way it walks.

    Inspector settings

    SpeedWorld units per second. 0 uses the baked walk's speed, or 3.2 with no baked walk. At the baked speed the feet stay planted walking left or right.
    Reads InputWalk with WASD or the arrow keys. Turn off to walk the actor from your own script.

  • Physics Body

    Inspector

    Simulates a baked actor in a Physics World during Play mode.

    Inspector settings

    Physics WorldThe Physics World this actor simulates in. The bake sets it from the archetype.
    Is StaticOn for scenery that physics never moves. The bake turns it on when the archetype's Movable is off (Advanced view).
    MassMass in kg, used when this object has no Rigidbody 2D. The bake sets it to the model's volume times the archetype's Density (Advanced view).
    SimulatedOff, the actor neither moves nor blocks in Play mode. A script can turn it off and on too.

  • Projectile

    Inspector

    Flies straight from where it was fired and damages the first Health it hits. A Projectile Blocker stops it.

    Inspector settings

    SpeedFlight speed in world units per second. At 0 it stays where it is until it hits.
    Max RangeHow far it flies before it is destroyed, in world units.
    DamageHit points it removes from the Health it hits.

  • Projectile Blocker

    Stops Sun Forge projectiles at this object's hitbox. The object takes no damage.

  • Shooter

    Inspector

    Fires at the pointer with a click, a touch or Space and plays the Play Once entry named Attack. Set While Walking to Keep Walking to keep the walk playing instead. Shots leave from the marker named Muzzle, else from the hitbox edge, and fly through the point you aim at.

    Inspector settings

    Projectile PrefabThe prefab each shot spawns. Empty fires a built-in projectile.
    Cooldown SecondsSeconds between shots while fire is held. Below the length of Attack, each shot restarts it. Just above it, Attack holds its last frame until the next shot.
    While WalkingWhat a shot plays while the actor walks. Play Attack plays the Attack entry in place of the walk. Keep Walking keeps the walk, played backward while the actor shoots one way and walks the other, and skips Attack and its Animation Events. It suits a walk that already holds the weapon up.
    Recoil PixelsUnder Keep Walking, how far a shot fired while walking pushes the drawn actor back, in the sprite's pixels. It recovers in a moment, and colliders stay where they are. 0 turns it off.
    Reads InputAim with the pointer or a touch, and fire with a click, a touch or Space. Turn off to aim and fire from your own script.
    Aim CameraThe camera the pointer aims through. Empty uses the camera tagged MainCamera.

  • Wander

    Inspector

    Roams around its starting point, pausing between walks.

    Inspector settings

    SpeedWorld units per second. 0 uses the baked walk's speed, or 0.4 with no baked walk. At the baked speed the feet stay planted walking left or right.
    RadiusHow far from its home point the actor walks, in world units.
    SeedThe same nonzero seed replays the same walk. 0 takes a seed from the home point.

Add Component › Sun Forge › Lighting

  • Canopy Shade

    Inspector

    Shades the ground and sprites under tree crowns, from a canopy map you draw or paint.

    Inspector settings

    MapCanopy map: white is open sky, black is under closed crowns. Draw Map draws one from your Trees. Only red is read, so a Single Channel, Red import saves memory.
    RectArea the map covers, in world units, relative to this object. Outside it the sky is open.
    Canopy HeightHeight above which sprites are out of the shade, in world units. Set it to about the tree height. Draw Map sets it to the average height of the treetops.
    TreesAn object that holds your trees and nothing else, for Draw Map. An empty object in it is a group and any other is one tree, whose pivot Draw Map reads as where the trunk meets the ground.

  • Cast Shadow Caster

    Inspector

    Makes a sprite cast its shadow, or the shadow you drew for it, under the sun.

    Inspector settings

    Shadow HeightOne height, in world units, for the whole shadow of something flat. Leave 0 for anything that stands. A shadow sprite with a cast height texture ignores it.

  • Cast Shadow Field

    Inspector

    Casts every sprite's shadow onto the ground and onto standing sprites, darkening overlaps once.

    Inspector settings

    Followed CameraCamera the field follows. Leave empty to follow the camera tagged MainCamera.
    Pixels Per UnitShadow resolution, in texels per world unit. 0 matches the finest Pixels Per Unit among the shadow sprites. A lower value costs less on a phone.
    ModeWhere shadows fall. Ground Overlay modes work on every ground material. Ground Material modes need Sun Forge styled ground, and the Lighting Setup's Cast Shadow Stretch lengthens their shadows. Modes ending in And Sprites also shade standing sprites that use styled materials.
    Sorting ModeLowest Shadow: where the lowest of the shadows sorts. Custom: the layer and order below. Decals sorted above show no shadow.
    Sorting Layer NameName of the sorting layer the shadow layer draws in. Empty uses Default.
    Sorting OrderOrder in layer the shadow layer draws at.

  • Cast Shadow Pair

    Pairs a sprite with the shadow sprite you drew for it.

    Inspector settings

    BodyThe body renderer the shadows follow. Empty uses the Sprite Renderer on this GameObject.
    Ground ShadowThe ground shadow renderer, or a shadow you drew, falling away from the sun. Empty if there is none.
    Ellipse ShadowThe ellipse shadow renderer. Empty if there is none.

  • Light Source

    Inspector

    Lights sprites around a point, along a line or over a rectangle, steady or flickering.

    Lights › Add A Light

    Inspector settings

    Driving Light 2DOn, this GameObject's Light 2D sets this light's color, brightness, Ground Radius and spot, so what drives the Light 2D drives this light.
    Driven By Light 2DOn, this GameObject's Light 2D sets this light's color, brightness, Ground Radius and spot, so what drives the Light 2D drives this light.
    ColorThe light's color. A flame reddens as it dips. In Flat, art close to a bright or saturated light can draw darker than with the light off: at dusk, at dawn, in a room and on a bright night. On a bright night, metal darkens under any color. Lower the light's Intensity or move it farther from the art to avoid this.
    Level
    IntensityBrightness at the light, on the same scale as the sun's intensity. It fades with distance and ends at Ground Radius. At 0 the light is off and takes no place among the lights a camera can light.
    Height Above GroundHow high the light hangs above the ground, in world units. Its pool lies on the ground below the GameObject, and a higher light lights it more evenly and more dimly. On art drawn for a camera elevation of 0 (a side view), a higher light sits farther from the art.
    Ground RadiusHow far the light reaches across the ground and anything raised, in world units, from the point below the light or from its line or rectangle. Height Above Ground does not change it. At 0 the light lights nothing. On art drawn for a camera elevation of 0 (a side view), the pool lies on the screen around the light and ends at this radius.
    Source RadiusThe radius of the light's glowing part, such as a flame, in world units. A larger radius gives softer shading on characters and objects, and wider highlights. A flame's light moves inside it.
    ShapeThe light's shape on the ground. Ground Radius reaches past its edge, and Inner Radius rounds it.
    Shape LengthThe light's length on the ground, in world units, along the GameObject's up axis. To aim it, drag the Aim handle in the Scene view or turn the GameObject.
    WidthThe light's width on the ground, in world units, across the GameObject's up axis.
    Inner RadiusInside this distance from the light's point, line or rectangle on the ground, in world units, the ground is evenly lit. At most Ground Radius, where the pool has a hard edge.
    Inner Spot AngleThe light is whole inside this angle, in degrees, around the GameObject's up axis. To aim it, drag the Aim handle in the Scene view or turn the GameObject.
    Outer Spot AngleThe light ends at this angle, in degrees, around the GameObject's up axis. 360 lights all around.
    KindA steady light or a flame that flickers in Play mode.
    Flicker AmplitudeHow deep a flame's light dips at its deepest. Most dips are shallower. 0 is steady.
    Flicker FrequencyHow fast a flame moves: about 10 for a candle and 3 for a campfire. Flutter sets how much of its flicker comes at this rate. 0 is steady.
    FlutterHow much of a flame's flicker is quick flutter rather than slow breathing. At 0 it only breathes.
    Glow RenderersRenderers that draw this light's glow. The light leaves glowing particles in the list unlit, and a Flame flickers their glow.
    Special Cases › Excluded ObjectsObjects this light does not light, such as the hero carrying it. An object covers every renderer under it, and a renderer only itself. Its glow in the mist stays.
    Special Cases › Is PrioritizedWhere more lights reach a camera's view than it can light, this light ranks ahead of every light without this option.
    Special Cases › Scaled By Scene LightsOn, the Scene Lights Intensity scales this light. Off, the light keeps its own brightness, even with Scene Lights at 0.
    Special Cases › Scaled By Emission Scale OfBrightens this light with the Emission Scale of this role's material in the scene's style. None keeps the light's own brightness.
    Special Cases › Light On Its Object ShiftHow much this light lights the object it belongs to, relative to the lighting style's Glow Light On Its Object: 0 keeps the style's, 1 lights it fully, -1 not at all. It changes nothing above 0 where the style already lights it fully, or below 0 where the style does not light it.
    Special Cases › Glow Parts Light ScaleMultiplies the lighting style's Glow Parts Light On Its Object for the object this light belongs to. 1 keeps the style's, 0 adds none. It changes nothing where the style's value is 0.

  • Lighting Setup

    Inspector

    Lights the scene with a sun or moon, sky, clouds and mist in its lighting style.

    Inspector settings

    Art ViewNo Art View is set, so each sprite, tilemap and sprite shape draws at the angle its material states. Pick From Above or From the Side to draw them all at one angle.
    Style › StyleThe lighting style this Lighting Setup applies: its materials, post-processing and lighting values.
    Style › Time Of DayThe hour the scene is lit for, from 0 to 24. Each value you have not changed on this Lighting Setup takes the style's value at this hour, and 12:00 gives the style's default values. The hour stays where you leave it, in Play mode too, until a script moves it.
    Global Light › Sun And Moon BrightnessMultiplies the sun's brightness by day and the moon's by night, at every Time Of Day. The rows under Values keep following the style's hours. 1 keeps the style's light. Light Sources keep their own brightness. Under the Flat style a value above 1 brightens little.
    Global Light › Sky BrightnessMultiplies the brightness of the sky's light, which fills the shade, at every Time Of Day. The rows under Values keep following the style's hours. 1 keeps the style's light. Under the Flat style a value above 1 brightens little.
    Global Light › Sun From Directional LightOptional. Empty uses this Lighting Setup's own sun. A Unity Directional Light here, such as the one that lights your meshes, sets the sun's direction, elevation, color and intensity. A Light 2D or a Light Source does not fit here. Styled sprites take its color as URP lights your meshes, and Sun Warmth tints it as it tints the Lighting Setup's own sun. Disabled, or aimed from below the horizon, it gives no sun: raise Night for the moon. Set its Culling Mask to Nothing if only the Lighting Setup should use it.
    Global Light › Sun ColorSun color. Sun Warmth shifts it warmer or cooler.
    Global Light › Sun IntensitySun brightness. 0 is no sunlight.
    Global Light › Ambient Sky ColorColor of the sky's light on every surface.
    Global Light › Ambient Sky IntensityBrightness of the sky's light, which fills the shade. 0 is no sky light.
    Global Light › Moon ColorColor of the moonlight, which lights the scene as Night rises. Moon Intensity sets its brightness.
    Global Light › Moon IntensityMoon brightness. 0 is a moonless night.
    Values › Follows Shadow SunTurn the sun and the moon to the bearing the shadows were cast from, so lit sides match them. While Sun From Directional Light holds a light, it turns the moon alone. No effect while the shadows disagree.
    Values › Sun › Sun DirectionSun bearing in degrees: 0 away from the camera, 90 right, 180 toward the camera, 270 left. Shadows do not turn with it.
    Values › Sun › Sun ElevationSun height above the horizon in degrees: 90 overhead, 0 at the horizon. It sets which sides the sun lights and how much light level ground gets. For night, raise Night. Shadows keep their length, so draw them longer with Cast Shadow Stretch.
    Values › Sun › Sun WarmthShifts the sun's color: 0 pale silver, 0.5 Sun Color unchanged, 1 amber. The moon is not affected.
    Values › Sun › Ground Shadow StrengthHow dark shadow sprites and a Cast Shadow Field's ground overlay are, higher for darker. They darken as the sun or moon strengthens against the sky and fade as it weakens.
    Values › Sun › Light Sources In SunlightHow fully Light Sources show in a brightly lit scene: at 0 only where a light is much brighter than the sun and the sky, at 1 as at night. In bright light, a Light Source only tints the art, which can darken pale art: to have lights brighten it, also lower the Lighting Setup's Sun And Moon Brightness and Sky Brightness.
    Values › Sky › Ground Bounce StrengthHow much light the ground bounces onto your sprites. 0 turns it off, and metal looks darker because it reflects no ground.
    Values › Sky › Ground Bounce Color
    Values › Sky › Canopy Shade StrengthHow much of the sky's light a Canopy Shade takes under tree crowns. 0 is no canopy shade.
    Values › Sky › EnvironmentWhat metal reflects, with no sun in it: the Lighting Setup draws the sun's highlight. None reflects a sky built from the sky and ground colors.
    Values › Sky › Environment IntensityBrightness of your Environment image on metal: 2 reflects it twice as bright, 0.5 half as bright.
    Values › Sky › Environment Follows Sun And SkyOn: the reflection brightens, dims and tints as the sun and the sky change, for an image of the outdoors. Off: metal reflects the image at its own colors whatever the sun and the sky do, for an image of a room.
    Values › Night › NightHow far it is into night: 0 lights the scene by the sun, 1 by the moon, and between, the sun fades into the moon. Materials turn to night vision with it. The sky keeps Ambient Sky Intensity at night, so lower it for a darker night.
    Values › Night › Moon DirectionMoon bearing in degrees: 0 away from the camera, 90 right, 180 toward the camera, 270 left.
    Values › Night › Moon ElevationMoon height above the horizon in degrees: 90 overhead, 0 at the horizon. It sets which sides the moon lights and how much light level ground gets.
    Values › Cast Shadow Stretch › Cast Shadow Stretch EnabledDraw ground shadows longer in the Cast Shadow Field, as a stylization. Off shows each shadow at its own length.
    Values › Cast Shadow Stretch › Cast Shadow Stretch Sun Angle OffsetLengthens shadows as if the sun stood this many degrees lower than the one they were cast under, down to 10 degrees. Long stretches draw softer. 0 draws no stretch.
    Values › Cast Shadow Stretch › Cast Shadow Stretch End OpacityHow dark the far end of a stretched shadow stays: 1 as dark as the shadow, 0 fades out.
    Values › Cast Shadow Stretch › Cast Shadow Stretch End SwayHow much the far ends of stretched shadows sway over time, as a share of the stretch. 0 holds them still.
    Values › Cast Shadow Stretch › Cast Shadow Stretch Body ClearanceHow far below its caster's top a stretched shadow stops landing on standing sprites. 0 can darken a sprite beside one just as tall, and 1 can leave the tops of plants unshaded.
    Values › Weather › Cloud StrengthHow much sunlight the darkest cloud shadow blocks, varying over time. 0 is no clouds. Art drawn for a camera elevation of 0 (a side view) takes none.
    Values › Weather › Weather Scroll DirectionDirection clouds and mist drift toward, in degrees: 0 away from the camera, 90 right, 180 toward the camera, 270 left.
    Values › Weather › Cloud Repeat LengthSize of the cloud pattern on the ground before it repeats, in world units.
    Values › Weather › Cloud SpeedHow fast cloud shadows drift over the ground along Weather Scroll Direction, in world units per second.
    Values › Weather › Cloud Variation Over TimeHow much cloud strength, drift and shape vary over time. 0 keeps them steady.
    Values › Mist › Mist EnabledShow ground mist, shaped by the settings below. Art drawn for a camera elevation of 0 (a side view) takes none.
    Values › Mist › Mist StrengthHow much the mist hides. Varies over time. 0 is no mist.
    Values › Mist › Mist CoverageAbout how much of the ground the mist covers with the shipped Cloud And Mist Texture. Lower values break it into patches.
    Values › Mist › Mist HeightHow tall the mist banks stand, in world units.
    Values › Mist › Mist ColorThe mist's color in white light. The sun, sky and Light Sources still tint it, and any color but white darkens it.
    Values › Mist › Mist Repeat LengthSize of the mist pattern on the ground before it repeats, in world units.
    Values › Mist › Mist SpeedHow fast the mist drifts over the ground along Weather Scroll Direction, in world units per second.
    Values › Mist › Mist Glow In DaylightHow much of a Light Source's glow the mist keeps in bright daylight. The glow fades as the sun and sky brighten, and is whole at night. 1 keeps all of it.
    Values › Glow › Glow Light On Its ObjectHow much a Glow light lights the object it belongs to. 1 lights it as much as the things around it, 0 not at all.
    Values › Glow › Glow Parts Light On Its ObjectHow strongly an object is lit by its own glowing parts, where its emission texture holds their light. 0 adds none. It adds to Glow Light On Its Object.
    Style Settings › NameThe style's name. Renaming it also renames its own materials and post-processing beside it.
    Show › Post Processing EnabledShow the style's post-processing while this Lighting Setup is in effect.
    Show › Sprite Emission EnabledShow the emission of styled sprites.
    Show › Ambient Occlusion EnabledShow the ambient occlusion of styled sprites.
    Show › Self Shadow EnabledShow the self-shadow drawn into styled sprites, such as a hat brim over a face. Turn off for a sun far from the one it was drawn under.
    Publish › Publishes Sun And SkyPublish the sun, sky and ground lighting. Turn off to publish your own from a script.
    Publish › Publishes CloudPublish the cloud shadows. Turn off to publish your own from a script.
    Publish › Publishes MistPublish the ground mist. Turn off to publish your own from a script.
    PerformanceWhat costs frame time or memory in the open scenes, largest first, and buttons that set those costs at once.

  • Lighting Style Renderer

    Inspector

    Gives a renderer you placed by hand the lighting style's material or one of your own, and lets you pick which colors of a sprite you drew glow or shine like metal.

    Custom shaders › Shader Graph Custom shaders › A Shader Of Your Own

    Inspector settings

    RoleSprite: actors, props and effects. Ground: terrain, roads, decals and other flat art drawn top-down. Ground Clutter: stones, tufts, grass and other small solids lying on the ground, drawn from the same angle as your standing sprites.
    MaterialA material of your own. Leave it empty to use the style's material for the Role.
    Height Above GroundHow far above the ground this sprite stands, in world units, such as a lamp on a table or a bird in flight. Its light, shade, cast shadows and the grass drawn over it are read at that height. It does not move the sprite: place it there yourself.
    Emission MultiplierMultiplies the material's Emission Scale for this sprite under every style, such as 4 for an emission texture stored at a quarter of its brightness. It rounds to the nearest step. Changing it here also scales the Glow light.
    Glow, Surfaces and EdgesMake colors of this sprite glow or shine like metal, by picking them on the sprite, and round its edges in the light.
    Glow, Surfaces and Edges › Glow Intensity
    Glow, Surfaces and Edges › Glow Light
    Glow, Surfaces and Edges › Round EdgesLights the sprite's outline as if it curves away from you, so its edges catch the sun on the side it shines from. The inside keeps the shading you painted.

  • Scene Lights

    Inspector

    Turns the brightness of every Light Source in the scene up or down with one dial.

    Inspector settings

    IntensityScales the brightness of every Light Source with Scaled By Scene Lights on. 1 keeps their own, above 1 brightens them and 0 turns them off.

  • Sorted Ground Cover

    Inspector

    Makes sprites stand in a grass sheet, with grass in front of their feet drawn over them.

    Inspector settings

    Fallback Geometry TextureGeometry texture for the grass sheet, used when its sprite has none.
    Height Range

Add Component › Sun Forge › Markers

  • Marker Motion Trail

    Inspector

    Draws a ribbon through one marker's positions on the frames before the one on screen.

    Inspector settings

    Marker › Followed RendererThe body renderer whose current sprite the trail follows. Empty uses the Sprite Renderer on this GameObject.
    Marker › Baked DataThe baked data that holds the marker positions. Empty uses the baked prefab's data.
    Marker › Marker NameThe name of the marker the trail follows, as authored for the bake.
    Trail › MaterialThe material the trail draws with, which sets its look. Empty draws nothing.
    Trail › History FramesHow many frames before the one on screen the trail reaches back, never past the clip's first frame.
    Trail › Clip NamesThe clips the trail draws on. Empty draws on every clip.
    Trail › Sort OffsetHow far in front of the body the trail sorts. Raise it if the trail flickers, lower it if it draws over a neighbor.

  • Marker Visual

    Inspector

    Draws a glow or sprite on one baked marker of the body, fading it as the model turns away.

    Inspector settings

    Marker › Followed RendererThe body renderer whose current sprite the marker follows. Empty uses the Sprite Renderer on this GameObject.
    Marker › Baked DataThe baked data that holds the marker positions. Empty uses the baked prefab's data.
    Marker › Marker NameThe name of the marker it follows, as authored for the bake.
    Look › MaterialThe material the marker draws with, which sets its look. Empty draws nothing, and only the light follows the marker.
    Look › Marker SpriteThe sprite a material with a main texture draws over the marker. A sprite in a Sprite Atlas with Tight Packing on draws nothing.
    Look › Light SourceA Light Source that follows the marker. On a frame with no marker position, a baked prefab's Glow light turns with the actor and any other light stays where it is.
    Sorting › Sort OffsetHow far in front of the body the marker sorts. Raise it if the marker flickers, lower it if it draws over a neighbor.
    Sorting › Draw OrderIn Front: always over the body. Behind When Turned Away: the body hides the marker once the model faces away.
    Sorting › Behind Body Facing ThresholdHow far the model must face away (0 side-on, 1 fully away) before a Behind When Turned Away marker sorts behind the body.

Asset types

  • Sun Forge Bake Archetype

    Inspector

    Holds the settings every bake of one kind shares, such as its output folder, camera and shadows.

  • Sun Forge Bake Archetype Library

    Holds a project's archetypes, which the Bake Window's Archetypes tab edits.

  • Sun Forge Bake Preset

    Inspector

    Holds one model's bake, such as its source, output name, clips, markers and behaviors.

  • Sun Forge Baked Data

    Inspector

    Holds everything one bake made, such as its sprites, directions and marker positions, for scripts to read.

    Inspector settings

    Baked Values › Output NameThe output name, which names the prefab and every sprite sheet.
    Baked Values › Archetype NameThe archetype the bake used.
    Baked Values › OutputsThe kinds of output the bake wrote.
    Baked Values › Direction CountHow many directions the rest pose and the particles were drawn from, counting the ones left out.
    Baked Values › Skipped DirectionsThe directions the rest pose leaves out, one bit each.
    Baked Values › Clip Direction CountHow many directions an animation with no directions of its own was drawn from, counting the ones left out. 0 uses the rest pose's.
    Baked Values › Clip Skipped DirectionsThe directions an animation with no directions of its own leaves out, one bit each.

  • Sun Forge Lighting Style

    Inspector

    Holds a lighting style's materials, post-processing and default lighting values.

    Inspector settings

    Sprite MaterialMaterial for renderers on the Sprite role: actors, props and effects.
    Ground MaterialMaterial for renderers on the Ground role: terrain, roads, decals and other flat art drawn top-down.
    Ground Clutter MaterialMaterial for renderers on the Ground Clutter role: stones, tufts, grass and other small solids lying on the ground, drawn from the same angle as your standing sprites.
    Post Processing › Post Processing ProfileThe post-processing the Lighting Setup shows under this style.
    Cloud And Mist TextureThe tiling texture clouds and mist drift over. Red is the sunlight a cloud lets through. Mist lies deepest where green is high.
    Default Lighting ValuesThe lighting every Lighting Setup on this style follows at 12:00, in each value not set on the Lighting Setup.
    Default Lighting Values › Global Light › Sun ColorSun color. Sun Warmth shifts it warmer or cooler.
    Default Lighting Values › Global Light › Sun IntensitySun brightness. 0 is no sunlight.
    Default Lighting Values › Global Light › Ambient Sky ColorColor of the sky's light on every surface.
    Default Lighting Values › Global Light › Ambient Sky IntensityBrightness of the sky's light, which fills the shade. 0 is no sky light.
    Default Lighting Values › Global Light › Moon ColorColor of the moonlight, which lights the scene as Night rises. Moon Intensity sets its brightness.
    Default Lighting Values › Global Light › Moon IntensityMoon brightness. 0 is a moonless night.
    Default Lighting Values › Sun › Sun DirectionSun bearing in degrees: 0 away from the camera, 90 right, 180 toward the camera, 270 left. Shadows do not turn with it.
    Default Lighting Values › Sun › Sun ElevationSun height above the horizon in degrees: 90 overhead, 0 at the horizon. It sets which sides the sun lights and how much light level ground gets. For night, raise Night. Shadows keep their length, so draw them longer with Cast Shadow Stretch.
    Default Lighting Values › Sun › Sun WarmthShifts the sun's color: 0 pale silver, 0.5 Sun Color unchanged, 1 amber. The moon is not affected.
    Default Lighting Values › Sun › Ground Shadow StrengthHow dark shadow sprites and a Cast Shadow Field's ground overlay are, higher for darker. They darken as the sun or moon strengthens against the sky and fade as it weakens.
    Default Lighting Values › Sun › Light Sources In SunlightHow fully Light Sources show in a brightly lit scene: at 0 only where a light is much brighter than the sun and the sky, at 1 as at night. In bright light, a Light Source only tints the art, which can darken pale art: to have lights brighten it, also lower the Lighting Setup's Sun And Moon Brightness and Sky Brightness.
    Default Lighting Values › Sky › Ground Bounce StrengthHow much light the ground bounces onto your sprites. 0 turns it off, and metal looks darker because it reflects no ground.
    Default Lighting Values › Sky › Canopy Shade StrengthHow much of the sky's light a Canopy Shade takes under tree crowns. 0 is no canopy shade.
    Default Lighting Values › Sky › EnvironmentWhat metal reflects, with no sun in it: the Lighting Setup draws the sun's highlight. None reflects a sky built from the sky and ground colors.
    Default Lighting Values › Sky › Environment IntensityBrightness of your Environment image on metal: 2 reflects it twice as bright, 0.5 half as bright.
    Default Lighting Values › Sky › Environment Follows Sun And SkyOn: the reflection brightens, dims and tints as the sun and the sky change, for an image of the outdoors. Off: metal reflects the image at its own colors whatever the sun and the sky do, for an image of a room.
    Default Lighting Values › Night › NightHow far it is into night: 0 lights the scene by the sun, 1 by the moon, and between, the sun fades into the moon. Materials turn to night vision with it. The sky keeps Ambient Sky Intensity at night, so lower it for a darker night.
    Default Lighting Values › Night › Moon DirectionMoon bearing in degrees: 0 away from the camera, 90 right, 180 toward the camera, 270 left.
    Default Lighting Values › Night › Moon ElevationMoon height above the horizon in degrees: 90 overhead, 0 at the horizon. It sets which sides the moon lights and how much light level ground gets.
    Default Lighting Values › Cast Shadow Stretch › Cast Shadow Stretch EnabledDraw ground shadows longer in the Cast Shadow Field, as a stylization. Off shows each shadow at its own length.
    Default Lighting Values › Cast Shadow Stretch › Cast Shadow Stretch Sun Angle OffsetLengthens shadows as if the sun stood this many degrees lower than the one they were cast under, down to 10 degrees. Long stretches draw softer. 0 draws no stretch.
    Default Lighting Values › Cast Shadow Stretch › Cast Shadow Stretch End OpacityHow dark the far end of a stretched shadow stays: 1 as dark as the shadow, 0 fades out.
    Default Lighting Values › Cast Shadow Stretch › Cast Shadow Stretch End SwayHow much the far ends of stretched shadows sway over time, as a share of the stretch. 0 holds them still.
    Default Lighting Values › Cast Shadow Stretch › Cast Shadow Stretch Body ClearanceHow far below its caster's top a stretched shadow stops landing on standing sprites. 0 can darken a sprite beside one just as tall, and 1 can leave the tops of plants unshaded.
    Default Lighting Values › Weather › Cloud StrengthHow much sunlight the darkest cloud shadow blocks, varying over time. 0 is no clouds. Art drawn for a camera elevation of 0 (a side view) takes none.
    Default Lighting Values › Weather › Weather Scroll DirectionDirection clouds and mist drift toward, in degrees: 0 away from the camera, 90 right, 180 toward the camera, 270 left.
    Default Lighting Values › Weather › Cloud Repeat LengthSize of the cloud pattern on the ground before it repeats, in world units.
    Default Lighting Values › Weather › Cloud SpeedHow fast cloud shadows drift over the ground along Weather Scroll Direction, in world units per second.
    Default Lighting Values › Weather › Cloud Variation Over TimeHow much cloud strength, drift and shape vary over time. 0 keeps them steady.
    Default Lighting Values › Mist › Mist EnabledShow ground mist, shaped by the settings below. Art drawn for a camera elevation of 0 (a side view) takes none.
    Default Lighting Values › Mist › Mist StrengthHow much the mist hides. Varies over time. 0 is no mist.
    Default Lighting Values › Mist › Mist CoverageAbout how much of the ground the mist covers with the shipped Cloud And Mist Texture. Lower values break it into patches.
    Default Lighting Values › Mist › Mist HeightHow tall the mist banks stand, in world units.
    Default Lighting Values › Mist › Mist ColorThe mist's color in white light. The sun, sky and Light Sources still tint it, and any color but white darkens it.
    Default Lighting Values › Mist › Mist Repeat LengthSize of the mist pattern on the ground before it repeats, in world units.
    Default Lighting Values › Mist › Mist SpeedHow fast the mist drifts over the ground along Weather Scroll Direction, in world units per second.
    Default Lighting Values › Mist › Mist Glow In DaylightHow much of a Light Source's glow the mist keeps in bright daylight. The glow fades as the sun and sky brighten, and is whole at night. 1 keeps all of it.
    Default Lighting Values › Glow › Glow Light On Its ObjectHow much a Glow light lights the object it belongs to. 1 lights it as much as the things around it, 0 not at all.
    Default Lighting Values › Glow › Glow Parts Light On Its ObjectHow strongly an object is lit by its own glowing parts, where its emission texture holds their light. 0 adds none. It adds to Glow Light On Its Object.
    HoursThe lighting this style sets at other hours of a Lighting Setup's Time Of Day. Each value blends from the nearest hour before that sets it to the nearest after, and 12:00 is the Default Lighting Values.
    QualityThe costly parts of the lighting this style draws at each quality level of Project Settings > Quality.

    Quality › Extra › Exact Shadows On Sprites

  • Sun Forge Physics World

    Inspector

    Holds a 2D physics world that baked actors share, which you pick in the archetype's PHYSICS section (Advanced view).

    Inspector settings

    GravityThe pull on every moving actor, in world units per second per second. A top-down game keeps it at 0.
    FrictionHow much actors grip what they slide along. 0 slides freely.
    BouncinessHow much actors bounce off what they hit. 0 stops dead, 1 keeps all the speed.
    Linear DampingHow fast an actor slows when nothing pushes it. Used only by actors with no Rigidbody 2D: the others take their Rigidbody 2D's Linear Damping when they enter the world.
    Unity Definitions › WorldUnity's settings for the world: gravity, simulation steps and contact tuning.
    Unity Definitions › Body DefaultsUnity's settings each actor's body starts from. Each body sets its own type and position.
    Unity Definitions › Shape DefaultsUnity's settings each actor's shape starts from: its surface, contacts and events. Each body sets its own mass, and each shape its contact category.
