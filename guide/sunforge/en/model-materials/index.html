Model materials

What a bake reads from your model's materials, and what each becomes in the sprite's textures.

On this page
  • Sprites And 2D Rigs
  • Models With Your Own Shader

How the model's materials bake:

  • A model's color texture bakes as its color, and your scene lights the sprite again, so light or shade painted into the texture shows in every sprite.
  • The material's Occlusion Map bakes into the mask texture. Use it for detail finer than the mesh, such as bark furrows.
  • A material that fades by alpha bakes cut at half opacity, so glass and other see-through surfaces bake either solid or open.
  • The bake draws both faces of your model's surfaces, whatever their material's Render Face, so a one-sided leaf card or cape seen from behind still shows in the sprite.
  • A mesh's own vertex colors do not bake, because URP's Lit and Standard ignore them.
  • A glowing part lights its own model only where it is in the open: a part pushed inside a solid lights nothing. A surface that renders one face is the skin of a solid, so for a glow behind one, such as a bulb under a lamp shade modeled as one layer of faces, set the shade material's Render Face to Both.
  • A clip that swaps the material of a mesh, sprite or line, or swaps a sprite with 2D Animation's Sprite Swap, bakes each frame with what that frame shows. Values a clip animates on a material, such as a color, a glow, a texture's offset or a cutoff, bake on every frame as the clip plays them.
  • A script does not run during the bake. On a scene object, values a script sets on its renderers with a MaterialPropertyBlock bake as the object shows them when you bake.

Sprites And 2D Rigs

  • A character rigged with 2D Animation bakes each frame in the pose its bones and 2D IK give it.
  • A sprite drawn with a Sun Forge lighting material bakes the normal, mask and emission textures it draws with, such as those Set Up for Lighting gives the sprite.
  • On a Sprite Renderer, each of the sprite's Secondary Textures bakes in place of the material's texture of the same name. In Shader Graph, the texture property of that name needs Show In Inspector on, or the bake cannot find it.
  • A Sprite Renderer's Color and a Line Renderer's Color gradient tint their part as they do in the scene.

Models With Your Own Shader

A shader of your own bakes from these properties:

The bake takes From
Color The texture and color the shader marks as main, or else _BaseMap or _MainTex and _BaseColor or _Color
Normal map _BumpMap or _NormalMap, scaled by _BumpScale as URP's Lit scales it
Glow _EmissionColor and _EmissionMap when the material's Emission box (the _EMISSION keyword) is on, or always if the shader has no such keyword

In Shader Graph, right-click a property and choose Set as Main Texture or Set as Main Color.

For a shader you cannot edit, a bake extension can give the bake's copy of the model a material that uses these property names (Extending a bake › Extensions). The Bake tab still names that material, because it checks the model before extensions run.
