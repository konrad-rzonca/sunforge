Sun Forge Documentation

Hover a field in the Bake Window or an Inspector for what it does. These pages hold what no field shows:

Page Look up
Output-Reference.md The files a bake writes, what a rebake keeps, characters in props, what a bake adds to the extra textures, and Addressables and version control
Baked-Lighting.md What a baked prefab adds to the lighting, and baking with a shader of your own
Scripting.md The assemblies, and examples for reading baked data, adapters, extensions, project defaults and baking from code

The Sun Forge Gizmos panel in the Scene view shows hitboxes, colliders, attach points, lights and more, one row each (Output-Reference.md, Adjusting A Prefab). A row shows only while the scene has something for it, and the panel hides while it has no row to show. To hide it for good, use the Scene view's Overlays menu.

The Getting Started sample has a README.md in its folder once you import it.

Packages

Package What it adds
Sun Forge The Bake Window, the components of a baked prefab, the gameplay components, and the Getting Started sample.
Sun Forge 2D Lighting The scene lighting, lighting styles, Light Sources and cast shadows, and the Lit Scene sample.
Sun Forge Showcase A playable scene built from baked output. Install it separately in the Package Manager. Its README says how to open it.

Sun Forge 2D Lighting installs with Sun Forge. Its pages, with an index.md that lists them, are in Packages/com.konradrzonca.sunforge.lighting/Documentation~/.

Tools > Sun Forge > Guide opens Sun Forge's pages online, with every menu, window and component and what each does: https://sunforgetools.com/guide/sunforge/0.3/en/.
