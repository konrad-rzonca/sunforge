Menus

Every menu item and shortcut Sun Forge adds to Unity, and what each one does.

  • Tools in the menu bar

Tools in the menu bar

Sun Forge

  • Bake Window

    Alt / OptionShiftB

    Opens the Bake Window, where you turn models into sprite sheets and a ready prefab.

    First bake

  • Set Up for Lighting

    After you confirm the changes it lists, lights the open scenes, or the prefab open in Prefab Mode, whatever is selected: gives their sprites lighting and cast shadows, and adds a Lighting Setup to a scene that has none.

  • Open Showcase

    Opens the Showcase, a playable scene baked with Sun Forge, and first downloads and imports it when it is not in your project.

    The showcase › Open The Showcase First bake › Showcase

  • Guide

    Opens this guide in your browser.

Right-click in the Hierarchy

Also under GameObject in the menu bar

Sun Forge

  • Open in Bake Window

    Opens the bake of the selected baked actor, or the selected scene object's prefab or model, in the Bake Window.

  • Set Up for Lighting

    After you confirm the changes it lists, gives the sprites of the selected objects, or of the open scenes or the prefab open in Prefab Mode, lighting and cast shadows, and adds a Lighting Setup to a scene that has none.

    First bake Set up a scene Lights › Use Your Light 2D Lights Side view

  • Camera Follow

    Adds a Camera Follow to the main camera to follow the selected object, and creates a main camera if the scene has none.

  • Cast Shadow Field

    Adds a Cast Shadow Field, which casts the sprites' shadows on the ground and other sprites.

  • Canopy Shade

    Adds a Canopy Shade, which shades the ground and sprites under tree crowns.

    Shadows › Canopy Shade

Sun Forge › Light Source

  • Light

    Adds a round Light Source with a steady glow to the scene.

    Set up a scene Lights › Add A Light

  • Spot Light

    Adds a Light Source that lights a cone down the screen.

  • Line Light

    Adds a Light Source that glows along a line.

  • Rectangle Light

    Adds a Light Source that glows over a rectangle.

Sun Forge › Light Source › Fire

  • Candle

    Adds a small Light Source that flickers fast, like a candle flame.

  • Torch

    Adds a Light Source that flickers like a torch flame.

    Side view

  • Campfire

    Adds a bright, low Light Source that flickers like a campfire.

  • Fireplace

    Adds a flickering Light Source that lights a half circle down the screen, like a fireplace.

Sun Forge › Light Source › Lamps

  • Lantern

    Adds a warm, steady Light Source at hand height, like a lantern.

  • Street Lamp

    Adds a high Light Source with an evenly lit pool, like a street lamp.

  • Neon Tube

    Adds a pink Light Source that glows along a line, like a neon tube.

Sun Forge › Light Source › Magic

  • Crystal

    Adds a small, cool Light Source near the ground, like a glowing crystal.

  • Magic Orb

    Adds a violet Light Source high above the ground, like a magic orb.

Sun Forge › Light Source › Windows and Doors

  • Window

    Adds a Light Source that casts a cool rectangle of light, like a window.

  • Moonlit Window

    Adds a Light Source that casts a pale blue rectangle of light, like moonlight through a window.

  • Doorway

    Adds a Light Source that casts a warm rectangle of light, like a lit doorway.

Sun Forge › Showcase

  • Make Flyer

    Makes each selected baked actor fly like the Showcase scene's bats.

  • Make Butterfly

    Makes each selected baked actor flit, bask and roost like the Showcase scene's butterflies.

  • Make Animal

    Makes each selected baked actor roam like the Showcase scene's animals.

Right-click in the Project window

Also under Assets in the menu bar

Sun Forge

  • Open in Bake Window

    Opens the selected model, prefab, bake preset, archetype library, baked output or output folder in the Bake Window, or the window as you left it when it cannot open the selection.

  • Set Up for Lighting

    Gives the selected sprites and prefabs, and those in selected folders, lighting and cast shadows, after you confirm the changes it lists.

The + button in the Project window

Also under Create in the right-click menu

Sun Forge

  • Physics World

    Holds a 2D physics world that baked actors share, which you pick in the archetype's PHYSICS section (Advanced view).

    Platformers and physics › Physics World

  • Behavior Script

    Creates a behavior script in the selected folder, for the Bake Window to add to baked prefabs.

    Behaviors › Writing A Behavior Platformers and physics › Side-View Games

  • Lighting Style

    Creates a lighting style of your own in the selected folder: a copy of Regular with its own materials and post-processing to tune.

The ⋮ button on a component in the Inspector

Also a right-click on the component's name

Baked Prefab › Reset Anchor

  • Collider

    Puts the Baked Prefab's Collider back to the size and place its bake gave it.

  • Glow Point

    Puts the Baked Prefab's Glow Point and its glow light back where the bake placed them.

  • Health Bar

    Puts the Baked Prefab's Health Bar back where its bake placed it.

  • Hitbox

    Puts the Baked Prefab's Hitbox back to the size and place its bake gave it.

  • Overhead Anchor

    Puts the Baked Prefab's Overhead Anchor back where its bake placed it.

  • VFX Box

    Puts the Baked Prefab's VFX Box back to the size and place its bake gave it.

Sprite Renderer

  • Set Up for Lighting

    Gives this sprite's object lighting and cast shadows, after you confirm the changes it lists.

Shortcuts

  • Sun Forge › Bake Window › Save

    Ctrl / CmdS

    Saves the preset or archetype library on the Bake Window's open tab, as its Save button does.

  • Sun Forge › Bake Window › Save As

    Ctrl / CmdShiftS

    Saves the preset or archetype library on the Bake Window's open tab as a new asset.

  • Tools › Sun Forge › Bake Window

    Alt / OptionShiftB

    Opens the Bake Window, where you turn models into sprite sheets and a ready prefab.
