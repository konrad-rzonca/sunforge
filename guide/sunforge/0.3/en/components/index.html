Components

Every component and asset type of Sun Forge, and what each one does.

Add Component › Sun Forge

  • Pixel Sort Character

    Inspector

    Sorts a character against each part of a prop it stands in, while Per-Pixel Prop Sorting is on.

    Output Reference › Characters In Props

Add Component › Sun Forge › Animation

  • Facing

    Inspector

    Holds the direction an object faces and shows its sprites for that direction.

  • Flipbook Animator Link

    Inspector

    Starts a baked particle flipbook's loop and turns it with the actor it sits under.

  • Flipbook Player

    Inspector

    Plays a baked actor's animations without an Animator, for scenes with many actors.

Add Component › Sun Forge › Baked

  • Baked Prefab

    Inspector

    Links a baked prefab's root to its bake and lets a rebake keep the components you added.

  • Baked Renderer Binding

    Inspector

    Links a renderer to its bake so Sun Forge shaders light its sprites with that bake's settings.

  • Baked Shadow Sync

    Keeps a baked actor's shadows on the same facing and animation frame as its body.

Add Component › Sun Forge › Gameplay

  • Camera Follow

    Inspector

    Makes a camera follow a target smoothly at any frame rate.

  • Health

    Inspector

    Takes damage from shots and strikes, and shows a health bar over the actor while it is damaged.

  • Melee Attacker

    Walks up to the nearest actor with Movement and strikes it, playing the Play Once entry named Attack.

  • Movement

    Inspector

    Walks with WASD or the arrow keys, facing the way it walks.

  • Physics Body

    Inspector

    Simulates a baked actor in a Physics World during Play mode.

  • Projectile

    Inspector

    Flies straight from where it was fired and damages the first Health it hits.

  • Shooter

    Inspector

    Fires at the pointer with a click, a touch or Space and plays the Play Once entry named Attack. Set While Walking to Keep Walking to keep the walk playing instead. Shots leave from the marker named Muzzle, else from the hitbox edge, and fly through the point you aim at.

  • Wander

    Inspector

    Roams around its starting point, pausing between walks.

Add Component › Sun Forge › Lighting

  • Canopy Shade

    Inspector

    Shades the ground and sprites under tree crowns, from a canopy map you draw or paint.

  • Cast Shadow Caster

    Inspector

    Makes a sprite cast its shadow, or the shadow you drew for it, under the sun.

  • Cast Shadow Field

    Inspector

    Casts every sprite's shadow onto the ground and onto standing sprites, darkening overlaps once.

  • Cast Shadow Pair

    Pairs a sprite with the shadow sprite you drew for it.

  • Light Source

    Inspector

    Lights sprites around a point, along a line or over a rectangle, steady or flickering.

    Lighting › Light Source

  • Lighting Setup

    Inspector

    Lights the scene with a sun or moon, sky, clouds and mist in its lighting style.

  • Lighting Style Renderer

    Inspector

    Gives a renderer you placed by hand the lighting style's material or one of your own, and lets you pick which colors of a sprite you drew glow or shine like metal.

    Custom Shaders › Shader Graph

  • Scene Lights

    Inspector

    Turns the brightness of every Light Source in the scene up or down with one dial.

  • Sorted Ground Cover

    Inspector

    Makes sprites stand in a grass sheet, with grass in front of their feet drawn over them.

Add Component › Sun Forge › Markers

  • Marker Motion Trail

    Inspector

    Draws a ribbon through one marker's positions on the frames before the one on screen.

  • Marker Visual

    Inspector

    Draws a glow or sprite on one baked marker of the body, fading it as the model turns away.

Asset types

  • Sun Forge Bake Archetype

    Inspector

    Holds the settings every bake of one kind shares, such as its output folder, camera and shadows.

  • Sun Forge Bake Archetype Library

    Holds a project's archetypes, which the Bake Window's Archetypes tab edits.

  • Sun Forge Bake Preset

    Inspector

    Holds one model's bake, such as its source, output name, clips, markers and behaviors.

  • Sun Forge Baked Data

    Inspector

    Holds everything one bake made, such as its sprites, directions and marker positions, for scripts to read.

  • Sun Forge Lighting Style

    Inspector

    Holds a lighting style's materials, post-processing and default lighting values.

  • Sun Forge Physics World

    Inspector

    Holds a 2D physics world that baked actors share, which you pick in the archetype's PHYSICS section (Advanced view).

Sun Forge Showcase

Add Component › Sun Forge Showcase › Brains

  • Showcase Animal Brain

    Keeps a wandering animal near its spawn point as the view turns, and puts it back on reset.

  • Showcase Butterfly Brain

    Flies a butterfly between flowers by day, startles it when someone comes near, and roosts it at dusk.

  • Showcase Coin Trail Brain

    Runs the coin trail, where collecting coins in order makes a crate pour gold and leave a chest.

  • Showcase Crate Stack Brain

    Lets the hero bump, push and shoot a crate off a stack until it bursts into coins.

  • Showcase Creeping Prop Brain

    Moves a prop toward the hero while the hero looks away, and freezes it when watched.

  • Showcase Cued Prop Brain

    Moves one coin, crate or chest when its game pulls, throws or bounces it.

  • Showcase Firefly Prop Brain

    Tips a rock aside to release fireflies when the hero lingers near it or a bolt flies past.

  • Showcase Flyer Brain

    Flies a bat around its roost and across the clearing, dodging the hero and tumbling when shot.

  • Showcase Skeleton Brain

    Raises a skeleton from the ground, walks it to the hero to strike, and raises it again after defeat.

  • Showcase Standing Animal Brain

    Keeps a standing animal breathing in place, turning it to stare at the hero who comes near.

  • Showcase Wisp Brain

    Runs the night wisps the hero gathers in the woods and leads to the shrine tree.

Add Component › Sun Forge Showcase › Debug

  • Showcase Debug Texture Viewer

    Shows the baked textures and their channels on every sprite when you pick one under DEBUG TEXTURE.

  • Showcase Health Bars

    Hides the health bars while a debug texture is shown and shows them again when you pick In Game under DEBUG TEXTURE.

  • Showcase Runtime Debug Draw

    Draws each actor's baked colliders, hitboxes, Y-sort pivots and markers when you turn on DEBUG OVERLAYS, with a cross at each bolt and where it hit.

Add Component › Sun Forge Showcase › Effects

  • Showcase Bat Swarm

    Flies bat flocks across the woods and gathers them around the hero when bolts hit enough bats.

  • Showcase Dissolve

    Dissolves the crate in step with the clouds.

  • Showcase Enemy Xray

    Outlines skeletons in red where trees or other sprites hide them.

  • Showcase Hit Reactions

    Shows the punch, damage number and spark when a bolt hits something.

  • Showcase Night Fireflies

    Fills the woods with fireflies one by one after dark while Gameplay is on.

  • Showcase Occlusion Fade

    Fades a tall prop out while it hides the hero, and back in when clear.

  • Showcase Skeleton Eyes

    Lights a skeleton's eyes as it rises, flares them on a strike, and darkens them as it falls.

Add Component › Sun Forge Showcase › Hero

  • Showcase Hero Controller

    Walks the hero with WASD, the arrow keys or a touch on the left of the screen, and turns the hero with each DIRECTION step.

  • Showcase Hero Light

    Sets the brightness of the hero's Light Source from the panel's Hero Light knob.

  • Showcase Hero Shooter

    Fires the hero's crossbow toward the pointer on a click, a touch on the right of the screen or Space, and draws the aim cue.

  • Showcase Muzzle Flare

    Flashes the hero's crossbow muzzle on each shot and fades it to dark.

  • Showcase Projectile

    Flies a crossbow bolt in a straight line and stops it at the first hitbox it touches.

Add Component › Sun Forge Showcase › Lighting

  • Showcase Ambient Occlusion Toggle

    Turns the baked ambient occlusion on and off from the panel's Occlusion chip.

  • Showcase Cast Shadow Field Controller

    Sets whether cast shadows fall on standing sprites as well as the ground, from the panel's Shadows row.

  • Showcase Day Night Cycle

    Runs a day and night cycle in Play mode while the panel's Gameplay chip is on.

  • Showcase Emission Toggle

    Turns sprite glow from baked emission maps on and off from the panel's Emission chip.

  • Showcase Lighting Style Controller

    Switches the scene between the lighting styles on the panel's Style row, and between a style's Outdoor and Indoor variants.

  • Showcase Scene Lights

    Sets the scene's light level from the Scene Lights knob, and darkens it while Emission is off.

  • Showcase Shadow Mode Controller

    Shows each actor's ground shadow, ellipse shadow or no shadow, as picked in the panel's Shadows row.

  • Showcase Sun Light Controller

    Keeps the panel's light controls and the scene's Lighting Setup in step, in both directions.

Add Component › Sun Forge Showcase › Scene

  • Showcase Actor Registry

    Keeps the list of actors the other Showcase components read, one per scene, and in the editor updates a moved object's z so it sorts by its new position.

  • Showcase Anchor Override

    Removes one placed actor's baked hitbox or moves its sort pivot, overriding its bake.

  • Showcase Bootstrap

    Starts the showcase's features when the scene plays.

  • Showcase Controller

    Holds the showcase panel's settings.

  • Showcase Direction Orbit Controller

    Turns the scene around the hero on each DIRECTION step, swapping sprites to the matching baked angle.

  • Showcase Low-Level Physics

    Switches the actors between Unity's low-level 2D physics and each prefab's own Rigidbody 2D, from the panel's Low-Level Physics chip.

  • Showcase Off Scene Reset Cue

    Lights the RESET button when the hero wanders off, and sends the hero home at the edge of the play area.

  • Showcase Panel Binder

    Fills the Showcase panel and the Asset Guide from the showcase's settings and scene, and lays the panel out for touch on phones and tablets.

  • Showcase Skip On Web And Mobile

    Leaves this object and its children out of scenes in Web, Android and iOS player builds.

  • Showcase Y-Sort

    Keeps a moving actor sorted by its ground position, so it passes behind and in front of props.

Asset types

  • Showcase Asset Catalog

    Lists the assets the Asset Guide shows, with their descriptions, credits and source links.
