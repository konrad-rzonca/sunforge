Baked textures

The textures a bake adds beside the sprite sheet, and how the sheets pack and import.

On this page
  • Extra Textures
  • Texture Settings

Extra Textures

Each extra texture is bound to its sheet as a sprite secondary texture. The lighting's page has the names and channels of the normal, mask, emission and Geometry textures (Sprites you drew › Sprite Textures). A bake adds these:

Texture Secondary texture name Channels
Mask _MaskTex A is 1 where the bake's sun lit the texel and 0 in the model's own shadow
Cast height _CastHeightTex On ground-projected shadow sheets with Bake Cast Height Texture (Advanced view) on. R × range is how far the tallest point casting each texel is above the pivot of the sprite that casts it. The body's pivot is on the ground. Texels the shadow does not cover read 0. The coverage is the shadow sheet's alpha. Linear

A sprite's normal map is drawn at the bake camera's angle, and a ground tile's is drawn top-down. range is the bake's height range.

A texture you bind to a sheet under one of these names is replaced while the bake writes that texture.

URP's Shader Graph Templates

A Shader Graph made from URP's 2D Sprite Lit or 2D Sprite Custom Lit template names its mask _MaskMap. Set that property's Reference to _MaskTex to read the baked mask. The Custom Lit template also names its normal map _Normal_Map: set its Reference to _NormalMap.

Unity's 2D Lights

On the Unity Lights material under the 2D Renderer, a Light 2D blend style masked on a channel lights a texel by that channel of the mask. Unity's stock Multiply with Mask and Additive with Mask blend styles read red, so they light a baked sprite only where it is metal. Point them at blue or alpha, or use the unmasked blend styles. A blend style masked on alpha also applies the self-shadow baked into the mask, which the Unity Lights material otherwise leaves out.

Grass Over A Baked Sprite

In a Geometry texture the bake writes, alpha is the frame's front edge (Geometry texture format). A Sorted Ground Cover draws a blade over the sprite only where the blade is rooted in front of that edge.

The edge is the front of the parts within 0.25 world units of the ground, so grass stops in front of a weapon held low. To let grass draw over a part an animation lifts higher, tick that animation under Measure Sizes From (Bake tab, ANIMATION, Advanced view).

Texture Settings

Set Filter Mode, Compression, Use Crunch Compression, Generate Mip Maps and Atlas Max Size in the archetype's OUTPUT section (Advanced view). A bake picks each texture's format on each platform from them.

  • The atlases pack when Sprite Packer Mode (Project Settings > Editor) is Sprite Atlas V2 - Enabled or Sprite Atlas V2 - Enabled for Builds. Under any other mode each sheet ships as a separate texture, and the Bake tab offers Use Sprite Atlas V2. After you change the project's Sprite Packer Mode, rebake what you baked before.
  • A Format of yours on a baked atlas's Default tab or on a platform's tab, or an Override at a Compression of None, replaces the bake's on that platform. Set Format back to Automatic and the next bake writes its own again. A Format you set on a Geometry texture or cast height texture stays.
  • A bake replaces a format that would draw a Geometry texture or a cast height texture wrong, on any tab, yours included, and says so in the Console.
  • With Generate Mip Maps (Advanced view) on, a sprite drawn at a quarter of its size or less lights its outermost pixel flatter and sorts it nearer the ground.
  • A smaller Max Texture Size on an atlas splits it into more pages and saves nothing.

A Web Build For Phones

On the Web, a bake follows Texture Compression on the Web platform (File > Build Profiles). At Use Player Settings, the default, it follows Texture compression format in Player Settings > Web. Under DXT, a browser without the DXT and BC7 formats, such as most phone browsers, unpacks each compressed baked texture to 4 times its memory. For a build that phones run:

  1. Set Texture Compression to ASTC. ETC2 also works, but with it a light close to a tall sprite can light it in bands.
  2. Bake again.

In Unity 6000.6, a build at Use Player Settings, or from a build script that names no format, can ship the sprite atlases' pages in an earlier format. After you change Texture compression format, select the sprite atlases and right-click > Reimport while Texture Compression is Use Player Settings.

Crunching One Platform

  1. Open that platform's tab on each baked atlas in the output folder, turn on Override and Use Crunch Compression, and leave Format at Automatic. While the atlases do not pack, do the same on the baked sprite sheets, not their normal maps or Geometry textures.
  2. Bake again. The bake keeps the atlas's normal maps, Geometry textures and cast height textures uncrunched on that platform.

A rebake keeps your override.
