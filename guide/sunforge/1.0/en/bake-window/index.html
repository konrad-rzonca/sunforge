SOURCE & OUTPUT

SourceThe 3D model (FBX or prefab) to bake.
Clip SourcesSpriteAnimator Controllers, model files and animation clips whose animations this bake can use besides the source's own.
+ Add Clip SourceSpritePick an Animator Controller, a model file or a clip whose animations this bake can use. You can also drop files on the list.
Choose Animations…Sprite
Output NameNames the output folder and files. Empty uses the source's name.
Prefab MaterialAdvancedThe material the baked sprites draw. While it reads Scene's Style, they draw the material of the style the scene's Lighting Setup has. Empty draws Unity's default sprite material, and on a ground tile the Ground material of the scene's style.
Target World HeightSpriteThe model's height above the ground, in world units. A lying log's height is its thickness. Auto keeps the model's own size. Its particles, collider, hitbox and anchors scale with it.
Target World Height › AutoSprite
Origin Below GroundAdvancedSpriteHow far the model's origin sits below the ground, in world units. Below zero lifts it in every frame, as a flame on a torch. For a creature that flies or leaps, use Leaves The Ground on its archetype. Auto stands it on its lowest point.
Origin Below Ground › AutoAdvancedSprite
Model YawAdvancedSpriteTurns the model before it bakes, so its front faces the way it moves. Turn it until the model faces you at rest in the preview.
Ground Tile SizeGround TileA ground tile's width, in world units. A width you set stays when Tile View changes, and the model scales to fit. Auto keeps the model's size.
Tiled Model SpacingGround TileSpacing of a ground tile's copies, in the model's units. Auto uses the smaller side of its footprint. Set it to move the copies closer or apart.
Bake ParticlesSpriteBake the Source's Particle Systems at the archetype's Animation Frame Rate (Advanced view), or 16 fps when that is empty. A looping effect repeats every Duration of its Particle System. An effect longer than 90 frames bakes fewer frames per second.
Override Max SizeAdvancedMax Size is in each sheet's texture import settings, and the bake sets it to fit the sheet. Off: a Max Size you set there stays, and a larger sheet is scaled down on import. On: every bake replaces it.

SECONDARY TEXTURES

NormalBake the normal texture, so lights shade the sprite's shape. Without it, Sun Forge materials light the sprite as flat.
MaskBake the mask texture: metal, gloss and ambient occlusion. Without it, Sun Forge materials use neutral values.
Mask › Metal And GlossWhere the metal and gloss come from: the material's own values, or the colors listed below. HDRP and glTF mask maps are not read.
Mask › + Add SurfaceAdd a color that bakes as its own metal and gloss.
Mask › Ambient OcclusionAdvancedUse the archetype's ambient occlusion, turn it off, or set it for this bake.
Mask › StrengthAdvancedHow dark the occlusion from the model's shape gets, from 0 (none) to 1 (full). Where the material's occlusion map is darker, the map shows.
Mask › RadiusAdvancedSpriteHow far occlusion reaches, as a share of the model's Drawn Height.
Mask › RadiusAdvancedGround TileHow far occlusion reaches, in world units.
EmissionBake the emission texture, so the parts that glow keep glowing in the game. In the Flat style, a glowing part shows its glow in place of its Sunlit Outline.
Emission › GlowWhere the glow comes from: the material's emission map and color, or the colors listed below.
Emission › + Add Glow ColorAdd a color that glows.
Emission › IntensityHow bright the colors listed above glow in the game.
Emission › Glow LightSpriteAdd a light where the model or its particles glow. The lighting style sets how it lights the model itself. Turn it off for props you place by the hundred.
Emission › LevelBrightness and reach of the light. For a fire or a lamp, raise it to about 1. The lighting style sets the light on the model itself.
Emission › KindFlame makes the light and the prefab's glow flicker in Play mode. For a fire, also set Level to about 1.
Emission › Flame › AmplitudeHow deep a flame's light dips at its deepest. Most dips are shallower. 0 is steady.
Emission › Flame › FrequencyHow fast a flame moves: about 10 for a candle and 3 for a campfire. Flutter sets how much of its flicker comes at this rate. 0 is steady.
Emission › Flame › FlutterHow much of a flame's flicker is quick flutter rather than slow breathing. At 0 it only breathes.
Geometry TextureSpriteBake each pixel's height above the ground, which lights, cast shadows, shade from trees and mist use. Off lights the sprite as a flat upright card.

ANIMATION

Rest Pose DirectionsAdvancedSpriteDirections the rest pose and its particles bake. Empty uses the archetype's. A prop's collider and glow follow these. More take longer to bake, and with no Idle entry use more texture memory.
Animation PlayerAdvancedSpritePicks what plays the prefab's animations and particles. The Flipbook Player costs less CPU when a scene holds many actors or many animated props.
Clips › DuplicateSpriteDuplicate this entry with a unique output name.
Clips › ModeSpriteThe role this entry plays in the generated Animator Controller.
Clips › ClipSpriteThe animation this entry bakes, from the source's animations. Rest Pose, or an empty Clip, bakes the model as it stands, with no animation.
Clips › RangeSpriteDrag to bake only part of the source clip. On a looping entry, start and end on matching poses, or the sprite jumps each time it repeats.
Clips › ReversedSpritePlays the Range from its end to its start, for example to make a get-up from a fall.
Clips › Extra YawSpriteTurns the model further for this entry only, for a clip that faces another way than the model.
Clips › RateSprite
Clips › DirectionsAdvancedSpriteDirections this animation bakes: a count and switches of its own. Empty uses the archetype's, or the Rest Pose Directions on a Rest Pose entry.
Clips › + Add Pose OverrideSpriteAdd bones that hold one pose of another clip while this entry plays, such as arms held in an aim pose over a walk.
Clips › + Add EntrySpriteAdd an animation entry. Its Mode is guessed from the clip's name. Several entries can bake one clip.
Clips › Measure Sizes FromAdvancedSpriteThe collider, hitbox, VFX box and health bar fit these animations when their Measure From is Animation Frames. Grass can draw over parts these animations lift more than 0.25 world units off the ground, whatever Measure From is.

MARKERS

BoneAdvancedSpriteThe bone the marker follows through every frame.
Local OffsetAdvancedSpriteMoves the marker off its bone, along the bone's own axes.
World SizeAdvancedSpriteThe marker's width and height in world units. Equal values make a circle.
OrientationAdvancedSpriteThe marker's rotation relative to its bone, in degrees. It sets the ellipse the marker makes on each frame.
ColorAdvancedSpriteThe marker's color, stored with the baked marker.
Occlusion GroupAdvancedSpriteMarkers sharing a group, such as a pair of eyes, fade on the group's far side as the model turns.

OUTPUT

KindAdvancedWhat this archetype bakes.
Tile ViewGround TileHow a ground tile is drawn: a square from straight above, squashed to Elevation Angle to match the sprites, or a flat diamond for an Isometric Tilemap.
Use Sprite Atlas V2Packs every bake into compressed atlases by setting Sprite Packer Mode to Sprite Atlas V2 - Enabled. Bake earlier output again. Your own atlases keep working.
Output RootFolder this archetype's bakes go in: a path starting with 'Assets', or one relative to the library's folder ('..' goes up). Leave blank to bake into the Sun Forge Folder.
Mark AddressableAdvancedRegister each baked prefab and its sprite atlases with Addressables. Needs the Addressables package 2.0.3 or newer.
Set Up AddressablesAdvanced
Addressables GroupAdvancedThe Addressables group a bake registers its prefab and atlases in. Creating it saves the project.
Addressables LabelAdvancedThe Addressables label each baked prefab and atlas gets, created if needed.
Atlas Max SizeAdvancedSpriteThe largest page each sprite atlas packs, on platforms you do not override. Larger pages can mean fewer draw calls, not sharper sprites, and some web and mobile devices cannot load a 16384 page.
CompressionAdvancedHow the sprite atlases and a ground tile's textures compress, on platforms you do not override. While the sprite packer is off, each sheet ships as a separate texture and compresses this way too. Below High Quality, a sprite that stands still can shimmer on Android and in ASTC Web builds.
Use Crunch CompressionAdvancedCrunch what a bake publishes, for smaller Web builds. Sprites lose some detail, and a sprite that stands still can flicker. Does nothing in ASTC builds, or on desktop when Compression is High Quality. Normal maps and heights stay uncrunched.
Filter ModeAdvancedHow every texture and atlas a bake publishes is filtered. Point keeps pixel art sharp. For hard edges, also turn off Supersampling in the QUALITY section.
Generate Mip MapsAdvancedGenerate mip maps for every texture and atlas a bake publishes, so a sprite drawn well below its size does not shimmer.

CAMERA

Pixels Per UnitSprite pixels per world unit: higher is sharper and uses more texture memory. In the Flat lighting style, higher draws finer crease lines, and a low value draws wider outlines.
Elevation AngleHow steeply the game camera looks down, in degrees: 0 is a side view, which bakes no ground shadows, and 90 looks straight down. For a side view use 0, not a small angle. Sprites are drawn from it, and every bake's lighting uses it.
DirectionsSpriteHow many directions the model is baked from, 1 to 64. 1 bakes only the view from behind, and 8 the four sides and four diagonals. More directions let a character turn closer to where it aims or moves, most of all toward the top and bottom of the screen. Animations and the rest pose can set directions of their own.
Directions › Fill Widest GapsSprite

QUALITY

SupersamplingAdvancedRenders up to 16 samples per pixel and scales them down, for smoother edges and detail that holds still between frames, at a longer bake, and fewer on a sprite over 1024 pixels on a side. Off renders one, which bakes faster and suits pixel art, though edges can flicker. Off with Shadow Softness at 0, Ground-Projected shadows are pixel-sharp.
Animation Frame RateAdvancedSpriteFrames per second for every animation entry that sets none, and for particles. Leave it empty to keep each clip's own rate and bake particles at 16 fps.
Ambient OcclusionAdvancedBake ambient occlusion from the model's shape into the mask. Where the material has an occlusion map, each pixel keeps the darker of the two.
AO StrengthAdvancedHow dark the occlusion from the model's shape gets, from 0 (none) to 1 (full). Where the material's occlusion map is darker, the map shows.
AO RadiusAdvancedSpriteHow far occlusion reaches, as a share of the model's Drawn Height.
AO RadiusAdvancedGround TileHow far occlusion reaches, in world units.
AO QualityAdvancedFast uses 32 directions and High 64. High shades more smoothly and bakes slower.

SHADOW

Bake ShadowsShade the model in its mask where it shadows itself under the sun, and bake the shadow types turned on below.
Sun ElevationHow high the sun sits, in degrees. A lower sun casts longer shadows.
Sun DirectionWhere the sun shines from, in degrees clockwise from the top of the screen. 180 puts the sun behind the camera.
SoftnessAdvancedHow soft a shadow's edge is. 0 gives a hard edge, pixel-sharp with Supersampling off and slightly smoothed with it on. On a sprite, this is the Ground-Projected shadow's fade width in world units. The shadow a sprite casts on itself softens as much, but stays sharper than the ground shadow at a high Softness or Pixels Per Unit. On a ground tile, the fade widens the farther the shadow falls from what casts it.
TintAdvancedSpriteThe color of the Ground-Projected and Simple Ellipse shadows. Its alpha sets how dark they draw under the Regular style's daylight, and Sun Forge's shadow material draws them darker or lighter with the light and the Lighting Setup's Ground Shadow Strength. A bake sets it as the Color of the prefab's shadow Sprite Renderers. A Color you set there is kept.
MaterialAdvancedSpriteEmpty uses Sun Forge's shadow material, which follows the light and fades under the mist. A Cast Shadow Field takes only the mist fade from it. Over ground that takes no mist, pick an unlit material such as Unity's Sprites-Default, which draws the Tint as it is. The In Game preview shows the sun and sky's effect, without cloud, mist or Light Sources.
Height RangeAdvancedSpriteThe height range the Geometry texture stores, in world units. Auto fits the model. Set it only if your code needs it fixed.
Ground-ProjectedSpriteBake the shadow the sun casts on the ground.
Resolution ScaleAdvancedSpriteShadow detail and texture cost, as a fraction of Pixels Per Unit. At 1 each shadow pixel is a sprite pixel, as pixel art needs.
Bake Cast Height TextureAdvancedSpriteBake the height each shadow pixel is cast from, so a tree's crown shades the sprites under it in a Cast Shadow Field. Off saves one texture per shadow sheet. With the sun from the side, the shadow then climbs only the bottom of the sprites it falls on.
Add Cast Shadow CasterAdvancedSpriteAdd a Cast Shadow Caster to the baked prefab, so a scene's Cast Shadow Field casts its shadow onto the sprites around it.
Simple EllipseSpriteBake a simple ellipse shadow under the model.
OpacityAdvancedSpriteHow dark the ellipse draws. 0 is invisible, 1 is the full Tint.
ScaleAdvancedSpriteScales the ellipse shadow relative to the model's top-down footprint.

PHYSICS

MovableAdvancedSpriteOn: a dynamic body when a Collider Shape is set, so physics moves it. Off: static, so physics never moves it. With no behavior that moves it, such as Movement or Wander, its Rigidbody 2D gets Linear Damping, so it slows to a stop after a bump.
DensityAdvancedSpriteDensity in kg/m³. The baked data's Mass is the model's volume times this, and the prefab's Rigidbody 2D or Physics Body gets that mass.
Leaves The GroundAdvancedSpriteOn: the prefab draws its body on a Body child that a script can raise and lower, while its collider, shadow and movement stay on the ground. For a creature that flies or leaps.
Height Above GroundAdvancedSpriteHow high the body stands above the ground when you place the prefab, in world units.
Collider ShapeAdvancedSpriteThe collider shape baked prefabs get. None adds no physics.
Collider Size › Measure FromAdvancedSpriteMeasure the rest pose, or the animations ticked under Measure Sizes From (Bake tab).
Collider Size › ViewAdvancedSpriteThe view the model is measured from.
Collider Size › Combine FramesAdvancedSpriteHow the measured frames combine into one rectangle.
Collider Size › Follows FacingAdvancedSpriteOn: the prefab uses the rectangle measured for the direction it faces. Off: it uses one rectangle, measured over every direction. Measured from the Rest Pose, each direction has one frame, so Combine Frames changes only the Off rectangle.
Collider Size › MultiplierAdvancedSpriteGrows or shrinks the rectangle.
Collider Size › Ignore Below HeightAdvancedSpriteIgnores geometry below this height in world units, such as legs or a base.
Collider Size › Ignore Above HeightAdvancedSpriteIgnores geometry above this height in world units, such as a tree crown or a raised weapon.
Add Physics ComponentsAdvancedSpriteAdd a collider to the prefab, and a Rigidbody 2D when Movable is on. Turn off if your game builds its own physics from the baked data.
Physics WorldAdvancedSpriteLeave empty for Rigidbody 2D physics. Assign one (Assets > Create > Sun Forge > Physics World) for Unity's low-level 2D physics.

HITBOX & ANCHORS

Hitbox › Measure FromAdvancedSpriteMeasure the rest pose, or the animations ticked under Measure Sizes From (Bake tab).
Hitbox › ViewAdvancedSpriteThe view the model is measured from.
Hitbox › Combine FramesAdvancedSpriteHow the measured frames combine into one rectangle.
Hitbox › Follows FacingAdvancedSpriteOn: the prefab uses the rectangle measured for the direction it faces. Off: it uses one rectangle, measured over every direction. Measured from the Rest Pose, each direction has one frame, so Combine Frames changes only the Off rectangle.
Hitbox › MultiplierAdvancedSpriteGrows or shrinks the rectangle.
Hitbox › Ignore Below HeightAdvancedSpriteIgnores geometry below this height in world units, such as legs or a base.
Hitbox › Ignore Above HeightAdvancedSpriteIgnores geometry above this height in world units, such as a tree crown or a raised weapon.
VFX Box › Measure FromAdvancedSpriteMeasure the rest pose, or the animations ticked under Measure Sizes From (Bake tab).
VFX Box › ViewAdvancedSpriteThe view the model is measured from.
VFX Box › Combine FramesAdvancedSpriteHow the measured frames combine into one rectangle.
VFX Box › Follows FacingAdvancedSpriteOn: the prefab uses the rectangle measured for the direction it faces. Off: it uses one rectangle, measured over every direction. Measured from the Rest Pose, each direction has one frame, so Combine Frames changes only the Off rectangle.
VFX Box › MultiplierAdvancedSpriteGrows or shrinks the rectangle.
VFX Box › Ignore Below HeightAdvancedSpriteIgnores geometry below this height in world units, such as legs or a base.
VFX Box › Ignore Above HeightAdvancedSpriteIgnores geometry above this height in world units, such as a tree crown or a raised weapon.
Health Bar › Measure FromAdvancedSpriteMeasure the top of the model on the rest pose, or on the animations ticked under Measure Sizes From (Bake tab).
Health Bar › Combine FramesAdvancedSpriteHow the tops of the measured frames combine into one.
Health Bar › Follows FacingAdvancedSpriteOn: the bar sits over the model's top in the direction the prefab faces. Off: it sits at one place, measured over every direction. Measured from the Rest Pose, each direction has one frame, so Combine Frames changes only the Off place.
Health Bar › WidthAdvancedSpriteThe bar's width in world units.
Health Bar › HeightAdvancedSpriteThe bar's height in world units.
Health Bar › PaddingAdvancedSpriteHow far the bar's center sits above the top of the model, in world units.
Overhead Anchor › Height On HitboxAdvancedSpriteHow high on the hitbox floating text such as damage numbers appears: 0 at the hitbox's bottom, 1 (100%) at its top.
