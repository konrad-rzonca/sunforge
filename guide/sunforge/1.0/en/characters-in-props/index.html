Characters in props

Let a character stand in a bush, with the part in front of its feet drawn over it.

On this page
  • Correcting One Prefab
  • Props That Stay Whole
  • Splitting With Your Own Shader

A character that stands in a bush can draw between the bush's parts: the part in front of the character's feet draws over it, and the rest behind it. To turn this on:

  1. Under Project Settings > Sun Forge, tick Per-Pixel Prop Sorting for each platform whose builds should split props. Props split in Play mode and in builds, and Play mode follows the active platform's value.
  2. Bake. The archetype's Pixel Sort (Archetypes tab, GAMEPLAY, Advanced view) says which prefabs are characters and which are props. On Automatic, a prefab is a character when it plays an animation or has a behavior that moves it, and a prop otherwise.

A character gets a Pixel Sort Character, and a prop's body sprite has Split Around Characters ticked on its Baked Renderer Binding. A script, such as an options menu, turns the split on or off for the rest of the run with SunForgePixelSort.Enabled.

Correcting One Prefab

To Do this
Keep a prop whole Untick Split Around Characters on its body sprite. A rebake keeps your choice
Split a prefab that only sways in place, such as a fern in the wind, around characters Untick its Pixel Sort Character. Untick it rather than remove it: a rebake keeps it unticked, and adds back one you removed
Split props higher or lower than the front edge of the character's collider Turn off Automatic Depth on the Pixel Sort Character and type a Depth
Split props around a character you built yourself, or around a baked prop your own script moves Add a Pixel Sort Character to it: Add Component > Sun Forge > Pixel Sort Character
Keep props whole around a character that you draw in front of them through its z or its own sort point Untick its Pixel Sort Character

Props That Stay Whole

A prop draws whole in every camera while a perspective camera draws its layer, and where it sorts on another sorting layer, order or render queue than the character. Only a few props split at a time. A prop that stays whole for another reason says why on its Baked Renderer Binding.

Check Setup on the Lighting Setup reports a perspective camera, and a sort axis the parts cannot sort along: a Custom Axis whose y and z have opposite signs.

Splitting With Your Own Shader

A prop drawn with a shader of your own splits only when the shader draws its share of the pieces:

  • A Shader Graph: wire the graph's Alpha from the Sun Forge Sprite Lit or Sun Forge Lit Color node, then tick Split Around Characters on the prop's body sprite. A bake leaves it unticked on a prop whose material is a graph with either node, because a graph whose Alpha skips the node draws the prop twice where a character stands in it.
  • A hand-written shader: follow the header of SunForgeWalkBands.hlsl (Custom shaders › Hand-Written Shaders). A prop whose shader does not take part draws whole.
