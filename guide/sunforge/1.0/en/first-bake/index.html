First bake

From your model to a baked prefab in your scene.

  1. Choose Tools > Sun Forge > Bake Window and drag your model or prefab onto the window. Or right-click the model or prefab in the Project window and choose Sun Forge > Open in Bake Window.
  2. Pick the Archetype that matches your model, such as Hero for the character you play.
  3. Check the sprite in the preview, beside your model. Drag to orbit.
  4. Click Bake. When it is done, click Show and drag the prefab it highlights into your scene.
  5. Choose GameObject > Sun Forge > Set Up for Lighting and click Apply. Baked prefabs with a lower Position Y then draw in front, and bloom and cast shadows show.
  6. Press Play. A prefab baked with the Hero archetype walks with WASD or the arrow keys and shoots toward the pointer when you click or press Space. The main camera follows it.

Showcase

For a playable scene built from baked output, with the 3D models and presets that baked it, choose Tools > Sun Forge > Open Showcase. If the Showcase is not in your project yet, Open Showcase downloads and imports it first.
