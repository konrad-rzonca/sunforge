Isometric

Build a stepped 2:1 isometric scene from baked blocks, and light ground tiles on a Tilemap.

To build a stepped 2:1 isometric scene from baked blocks:

  1. Keep each archetype's Elevation Angle (CAMERA section) at 30, which draws a square of ground as a 2:1 diamond, and give them all one Pixels Per Unit.
  2. Add a Grid with Cell Layout set to Isometric and a Cell Size of your diamond's width, half that width, and 1.
  3. Model each terrain block with a corner toward the camera. Bake it as a prop with Directions at 1 and SHADOW off, turn off its Cast Shadow Caster, and place it at Grid.GetCellCenterWorld.
  4. To stand a prefab on a raised block, set Height Above Ground on each of its Baked Renderer Binding components to the block's height, then call SunForgeBakedShadowSync.PlaceAtHeight, which moves it and its shadows up. Move a prefab with no Baked Shadow Sync, such as a particle bake, and a prefab baked with Leaves The Ground up by hand instead: add the block's height times the cosine of the Elevation Angle, 0.866 at 30, to its y position. The Body child's Height Above Ground stays its height above the block.
  5. Set each block's Order in Layer to -10 times (x + y) of its cell, so nearer cells draw on top. Give anything on that cell a value above its block's and below the next row's.
  6. Add the height a Light Source stands on to its Height Above Ground. Its pool keeps its Ground Radius on the block, and the Scene view's outline stays at ground height.
  7. Set the Cast Shadow Field's Mode to Ground Material And Sprites, so shadows fall on the blocks and on what stands on them.
  8. To paint the lowest level instead of building it from blocks, draw it on a Tilemap of ground tiles (Isometric › Ground Tiles On A Tilemap) and set the Tilemap's Order in Layer below every block's. Its tiles light as the tops of the blocks do.

Ground Tiles On A Tilemap

  • Draw the Tilemap with your style's Ground material, SunForge_<Style>_Ground.
  • A Tilemap needs a Baked Renderer Binding to light its tiles as their prefab does. Check Setup adds it, and reports a Tilemap whose tiles come from bakes that differ. Put those on Tilemaps of their own.
  • A tile with Tile View (Archetypes tab, OUTPUT) set to Isometric has a diamond sprite mesh, which a Tilemap needs to fit its cells. Every bake writes it, so Mesh Type does not keep another value.
