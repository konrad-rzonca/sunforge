Platformers and physics

Walk and jump a baked actor in a side view with a script of your own, and run baked prefabs in a low-level 2D physics world.

On this page
  • Side-View Games
  • Physics World

Side-View Games

Movement, Wander and the Melee Attacker move an actor up and down the screen as well as across it, with no gravity, like a flyer or a swimmer. To walk an actor that has Movement only left and right, turn its ReadsInput off and each frame call SunForgeMovement.Walk(direction, Time.deltaTime) with a direction whose y is 0.

A platformer's bake settings are in Side view › Side-View Bakes. Move its actor, and any actor that stays on its floor, with a script of your own:

  1. Start the script with Assets > Create > Sun Forge > Behavior Script, add movesActor: true to its [SunForgeBehavior] and list it in GAMEPLAY (Advanced view) in place of Movement, Wander or the Melee Attacker, so the bake sets the Rigidbody 2D's Linear Damping to 0. A script you add to the prefab yourself needs Linear Damping at 0.
  2. Give the actor gravity: raise Gravity Scale on its Rigidbody 2D, which a rebake keeps. With a Physics World, set gravity in its world definition.
  3. In the script, set the Rigidbody 2D's horizontal velocity from your input, and its vertical velocity only to jump. With a Physics World, write the Velocity of the actor's SunForgePhysicsBody.
  4. Each frame, standing still included, call SunForgeFacing.FaceUnlessHeld with the direction the actor walks and Time.deltaTime, and SunForgeActorAnimation.Of(this).SetMoveSpeed with 1 while it walks and 0 at rest. A Shooter whose While Walking is Keep Walking needs both: without SetMoveSpeed it plays Attack on every shot, and without FaceUnlessHeld its walk never plays backward while the actor shoots one way and walks the other.
  5. To play a jump, call SetBool(Animator.StringToHash("Jump"), true) on the same SunForgeActorAnimation. It plays the Loop While Bool entry named Jump until you set it false.
  6. The Shooter also fires on Space. If your script jumps on Space, turn the Shooter's Reads Input off, aim it with AimAt or AimDirection, and call its Fire from your script.

Physics World

To run baked prefabs in a Unity low-level 2D physics world, create one with Assets > Create > Sun Forge > Physics World, set its world, body and shape definitions in its Inspector, and name it in the archetype's PHYSICS section (Advanced view). Each prefab with a Collider Shape then gets a SunForgePhysicsBody.

To build your own physics shapes from the baked data, read SunForgeBakedPrefab.Anchors, and read it again each time SunForgeBakedPrefab.AnchorsChanged fires. An actor whose collider has Follows Facing on (Archetypes tab, PHYSICS, Advanced view) changes its collider as it turns.
