Requirements

What your project needs before you bake or light a scene.

  • Unity 6.3 LTS (6000.3.29f1 or newer) through Unity 6.6.
  • The Universal Render Pipeline, with the 2D Renderer or the Universal Renderer. The Built-in Render Pipeline and HDRP are not supported.
  • The Linear color space (Project Settings > Player > Other Settings > Color Space).
  • To bake, Force Text asset serialization (Project Settings > Editor > Asset Serialization > Mode), the default for a new Unity project.
  • On Windows, a project folder path of 110 characters or fewer. In a longer one, some files go over Windows' path limit and fail to import or bake.
  • A 2D scene seen by a camera that faces your sprites straight on, with Rotation X and Y at 0 in its Transform:
    • Top-down, isometric and side-view games all fit: the bake draws the angle into the sprites, so the camera itself never tilts toward the ground.
    • The camera can move, zoom and shake, and turn to any Rotation Z, such as for a Dutch angle, and the light and shadows keep their place in the world.
    • A Perspective camera, such as one for parallax layers, lights the scene the same. Only the shadows drawn as sprites show under it, and a character standing in a bush draws wholly in front of it or behind it (Characters in props).
    • For a 3D scene, or a camera tilted toward the ground, set the bake's Prefab Material (Bake tab, Advanced view) to Unity Lights and use the Universal Renderer. The Unity Lights material is lit by URP's lights and draws no mist, cast shadows or ground cover (Styles › Materials).
  • Every effect runs on WebGL 2 and OpenGL ES 3.0 players. On a device that cannot blend into 16-bit render textures, a Cast Shadow Field on Ground Overlay And Sprites, Ground Material And Sprites or Ground Material Only turns off. Shadows drawn as sprites still show, and a sprite without one casts no shadow.
  • Installing Sun Forge also installs Sun Forge 2D Lighting, URP, the Render Pipelines Core package, Burst, the Input System and the 2D Sprite package, and enables the Animation, JSONSerialize, Particle System and Physics 2D built-in packages.
