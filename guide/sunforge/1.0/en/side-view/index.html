Side view

Light art drawn from the side, such as a platformer's, and bake an actor for it.

On this page
  • Lights In A Side View
  • What A Side View Leaves Out
  • Side-View Bakes

Art drawn from the side, such as a platformer's, is a side view. To light a side-view scene:

  1. Choose GameObject > Sun Forge > Set Up for Lighting.
  2. In the window's first row, Art View, pick From the Side, then click Apply. Your sprites, floors and walls then light as a side view, under any style and material.
  3. On the Lighting Setup, set Sun Direction and Sun Elevation so the sun comes from the side.
  4. For a torch or a fire, add a light with GameObject > Sun Forge > Light Source > Fire > Torch. If the sun drowns its pool by day, raise the light's Intensity, and under the Flat style also the Lighting Setup's Light Sources In Sunlight.

Art View is also on the Lighting Setup, where you can change it later. Each scene's Lighting Setup has its own, so one project can hold side-view and top-down levels.

Lights In A Side View

  • Height Above Ground is how far in front of the art a light stands, and Ground Radius how far its light reaches across the screen. For a lamp on a wall or in a hand, set a small Height Above Ground, however high the lamp hangs.
  • A light lights every side-view sprite within its reach, a backdrop included. To keep a backdrop unlit, add it to the light's Excluded Objects.
  • A sprite at a smaller Z than a light, such as a character walking past a wall torch, is in front of it: the light reaches only the sprite's edges, and in Flat none of the sprite. To light the sprite's front, give the light the sprite's Z or a smaller one.

What A Side View Leaves Out

  • Cloud shadows, canopy shade, mist, ground cover, cast shadows, Cast Shadow Stretch, Light From Lit Ground and Exact Shadows On Sprites do not show, because a side view shows no ground for them to lie on.
  • A sprite Sun Forge rendered at an angle takes all of these except cloud shadows, canopy shade and mist. Use From Above for a scene of such sprites.

Side-View Bakes

To bake an actor for a side-view game, such as a platformer:

  1. Set the archetype's Elevation Angle (Archetypes tab, CAMERA) to 0.
  2. Set Directions to 4 and switch off the directions facing 0° and 180°, so the actor faces left or right and starts facing right.
  3. For a collider that covers the whole body rather than the feet, press Cover Whole Body under Elevation Angle, or in the PHYSICS section (Advanced view) set the collider's Ignore Below Height to 0, clear its Ignore Above Height and set its Multiplier to 1.

For a platformer, move the actor with a script of your own (Platformers and physics › Side-View Games).
