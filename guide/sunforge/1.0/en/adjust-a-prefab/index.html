Adjust a prefab

Move a baked prefab's hitbox, collider, health bar, glow point and facing in the Scene view.

Select a baked prefab, or a copy of it in a scene, to adjust its hitbox, collider, health bar, VFX box, overhead anchor, glow point and facing in the Scene view:

  1. In the Sun Forge Gizmos panel in the Scene view, click a row's name to turn it on: Collider, Hitbox, Attach Points for the health bar, VFX box and overhead anchor, Glow Point or Facing. Edit beside it in the Inspector does the same: under Anchors on the Baked Prefab, or on Facing.
  2. Drag its handles. To type a value or put it back, unfold it under Anchors on the Baked Prefab and use its fields or Reset. For the facing, use Angle on Facing.

A rebake keeps your adjustments.

Click the small icon at a row's right end for All in View: the Scene view then shows the hitbox or the health bar of every prefab that uses one or that you adjusted. Select a prefab to see and drag its own.

Moving A Marker

A marker is set by the bake, so it has no handle in the Scene view:

  1. Click Open in Bake Window on the prefab's Inspector, which opens the bake that made it.
  2. To move a marker, turn on Markers in the preview's Gizmos menu and drag it. To add one, add it in the MARKERS section (Bake tab, Advanced view) and pick its Bone.
  3. Bake again.
