Behaviors

Write a behavior the Bake Window can add, and receive animation events.

On this page
  • Writing A Behavior
  • Animation Events

Writing A Behavior

Start a behavior with Assets > Create > Sun Forge > Behavior Script. Once Unity compiles it, the + Add Behavior picker (GAMEPLAY, Advanced view) lists it under Your Project, named as its [AddComponentMenu] names it, else by its class name.

  • Keep the SunForgeBakedPrefab that SunForgeBakedPrefab.Of(this) returns and read its Data when you need it, so a later SetData reaches your script.
  • For a projectile of your own, SunForgeHitSweep.TryFirstHit returns the first SunForgeHealth or SunForgeProjectileBlocker that its move hits.

Moving The Actor

  • A script that moves the actor adds movesActor: true to its [SunForgeBehavior]. Without it, the bake gives a movable prefab Linear Damping, which slows the actor.
  • SunForgeLocomotion walks the actor for a mover of your own, turns it the way it walks and plays its walk animation. Call its ClearCachedAnimator from OnEnable, so an actor whose visual you swapped plays the new one.
  • Movement reads only the keyboard. To walk its actor from a touch control, or to a point, turn its ReadsInput off and each frame call SunForgeMovement.Walk or SunForgeMovement.WalkTowards.
  • After you swap the visual of an actor that has Movement, Wander, the Melee Attacker or the Shooter, disable and enable the actor, so they play the new one.

Playing Animations

  • Play animations through SunForgeActorAnimation.Of(this), whose triggers, bools, Speed and restart work under whichever player the bake's Animation Player (Advanced view) picks.
  • SunForgeActorAnimation.BakedParticlesOf(this) returns what plays the actor's particles: its Play(SunForgeAnimatorParameters.BakedParticlesState, t), with t from 0 to 1, starts or replays them. Activate a pooled effect before you play it.
  • To learn when an animation has finished, start it with PlayTracked or SetTriggerTracked. Each returns a SunForgeAnimationWait: read its IsDone, or yield return it in a coroutine.
  • To run a method at a point of an animation, give its clip an Animation Event (Behaviors › Animation Events).

Animation Events

An Animation Event in a source clip fires in the baked animation as the frame that shows its pose appears, whichever player Animation Player (Advanced view) picks. It calls the method of that name on each script on the prefab's root. Add a script with that method to the prefab's root, or list one under GAMEPLAY (Advanced view).

  • An event outside the entry's Range is left out.
  • An event between two frames fires on the nearer one, so at a lower Frame Rate two events one source frame apart can fire together.
  • In a looping animation, an event in the last half-frame fires on the first frame, so it fires as each loop starts, the first one included.
  • When an animation has events, its state in the controller has a Speed of 2, and the controller has a SunForge:Events parameter at 1. Leave both: they play the animation at its baked rate, with its events.
  • Under Flipbook Player, and on a prefab baked with Leaves The Ground (Archetypes tab, PHYSICS, Advanced view), a method that takes an AnimationEvent gets its function name, parameters and time. Reading its Animator clip or state info logs an error, so check isFiredByAnimator first. Under either, a method that takes an AnimationEventInfo is not called. On a prefab baked with Leaves The Ground under an Animator, the events of a clip of your own fire on the Body child.
