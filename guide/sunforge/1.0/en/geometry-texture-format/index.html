Geometry texture format

What each channel of a Geometry texture holds, for a shader or a tool of your own that reads or writes one.

A Geometry texture holds each sprite pixel's height above the pivot in red, and in alpha where the sprite meets the ground. To paint one, see Sprites you drew › Painting A Geometry Texture.

The Format

  • Height above the pivot is R * range * scale. Black is the pivot, white is the range, and nothing lies below the pivot.
  • range is the range a component on the renderer sets, where one does, and otherwise the material's Height Range (World Units). SFBakeHeightRange in SunForgeRendererBakeValues.hlsl reads it. scale is the object's scale.
  • A texel with alpha of 8/255 or more has a height. A texel below that has none: a standing sprite draws there as a flat upright card, and a ground sprite at height 0.
  • Green and blue are reserved and no material reads them, so a gray map, a red-only map and a single-channel map with the same red read alike. Do not read green or blue in your own shader.
  • Save the map lossless, such as PNG. The texture is linear, uncrunched, and filtered as its sheet is.
  • Any texture format works that keeps red and moves alpha by at most 4/255. A compressed format keeps fewer heights, so Check Setup imports a painted map uncompressed.

Sun Forge's ground cover also reads alpha as the footprint's front edge: how far in front of the pivot the sprite meets the ground. Grass draws over the sprite only in front of that edge. If you don't use ground cover, ignore this table.

Alpha Front edge
243/255 to 255/255 At the pivot
242/255 down to 27/255 (n / 25)^2 of the range in front of the pivot, where n is (247 - a) / 9 rounded to the nearest whole number and a is alpha from 0 to 255
8/255 to 26/255 The full range in front of the pivot
Below 8/255 No height

To store an edge, set a to 247 - 9 * n, where n is 25 * sqrt(edge / range) rounded, at most 25, or to 255 when n is 0. Alpha up to 4/255 above or below the value you set reads the same edge.

To read a texel, use SFHeightShare, SFHeightAbsent and SFHeightFrontEdgeShare in SunForgeHeightDecode.hlsl, or SunForgeHeightTexel.TryDecode in C#.
