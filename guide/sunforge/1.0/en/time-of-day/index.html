Time of day

Setting a scene's hour, and what changes with it from dawn to night.

On this page
  • Values You Changed
  • Change A Style's Hours
  • Run A Day From A Script

Time Of Day, under Style on the Lighting Setup, sets the hour, from 0 to 24. Each value follows the style at that hour. Every shipped style has a dawn, a morning, an afternoon, a dusk and a night from 20:00 to 04:00. Under an Indoor style, the light through the windows warms and dims at dawn and dusk, and at night the moon and your Light Sources light the room.

The values the shipped styles change with the hour:

Value What it changes
Sun Elevation Which sides the sun lights, and how bright level ground is
Sun Color, Sun Intensity, Sun Warmth The sunlight's color and brightness
Ambient Sky Color, Ambient Sky Intensity The light from the sky, in shade and on every side
Night Fades the sun into the moon and turns materials to night vision
Cast Shadow Stretch Enabled, Cast Shadow Stretch Sun Angle Offset Longer ground shadows at dawn and dusk, under Regular
Ground Shadow Strength How dark shadow sprites and a Cast Shadow Field's ground overlay are

Values You Changed

A value you change on the Lighting Setup keeps your value at every hour. A value that follows the hours keeps following when you set it to what the style already gives it at that hour. The revert button on a value's row, or Revert All in the header of Global Light or Values, gives a value back to the hours. To make the scene brighter or darker and keep its values following the hours, set Sun And Moon Brightness and Sky Brightness under Global Light instead.

To animate a value that follows the hours, add its curve in the Animation window, or in an editor script with AnimationUtility.SetEditorCurve. A clip built with AnimationClip.SetCurve moves a value only after you change it on the Lighting Setup or call SunForgeLightingSetup.KeepValue on it.

Change A Style's Hours

  1. Select your Lighting Setup and open the section named for its style, below Values. On one of Sun Forge's styles, click Edit A Copy in the section's header.
  2. In that section, open Hours. It lists 12:00, which opens the style's Default Lighting Values, then a key for each other hour the style sets. In a key, select the checkbox of each value it sets at its hour.
  3. Click Add Hour for a new key. Turn on Held for a key that holds its values over a span of hours.

Run A Day From A Script

To run a day from a script, set SunForgeLightingSetup.TimeOfDay in Update.

A script that sets a value to what the style already gives it at that hour keeps it at every hour with SunForgeLightingSetup.KeepValue, such as setup.KeepValue(nameof(SunForgeLightingValues.MistEnabled)).
